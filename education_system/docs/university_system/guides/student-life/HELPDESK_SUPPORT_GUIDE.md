## Creating Support Tickets

### Submitting a Ticket (Students)

**Via GUI:**
1. Navigate to **Student Support** → **Create Ticket**
2. Fill ticket form:
   - **Category**: Academic, Financial, Technical, Housing, Health, Other
   - **Subject**: Brief description (50 chars)
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation of issue
   - **Priority**: Low, Normal, High, Urgent
   - **Preferred Contact**: Email, Phone, In-person
3. Attach files (screenshots, documents) - max 10MB
4. Click **Submit**
5. Receive ticket number via email

**Via Email:**
1. Send email to `support@university.edu`
2. Subject line becomes ticket subject
3. Email body becomes description
4. Attachments automatically included
5. Auto-reply with ticket number

**Via Phone:**
1. Call helpdesk: (555) 123-HELP
2. Agent creates ticket during call
3. Receive confirmation email with details

### Ticket Categories

| Category | Examples | Typical Response Time |
|----------|----------|----------------------|
| **Academic** | Grades, enrollment, course issues | 1 business day |
| **Financial** | Billing, financial aid, payments | 2 business days |
| **Technical** | IT issues, account access, systems | 4 hours |
| **Housing** | Room assignments, maintenance | 1 business day |
| **Health** | Medical concerns, counseling requests | Same day |
| **Administrative** | Records, transcripts, forms | 2 business days |
| **General** | Other inquiries | 1 business day |

### Ticket Priority Levels

- **Low**: General questions, no deadline
- **Normal**: Standard request, routine issue
- **High**: Urgent issue affecting academics/wellbeing
- **Critical**: Emergency requiring immediate attention

**Auto-escalation:**
- Certain keywords trigger high priority (abuse, suicide, emergency)
- System alerts supervisor for critical tickets
- Crisis protocol activated automatically

## Ticket Management

### Viewing Your Tickets (Students)

1. Navigate to **My Tickets**
2. View ticket list with:
   - Ticket number, subject, status
   - Created date, last update
   - Assigned agent
3. Click ticket to view full details

**Ticket Statuses:**
- **Open**: Newly created, awaiting assignment
- **In Progress**: Agent actively working on it
- **Waiting on Student**: Needs your response
- **Waiting on Third Party**: External dependency
- **Resolved**: Issue solved, awaiting confirmation
- **Closed**: Ticket completed and confirmed

### Responding to Tickets

**Adding Comments:**
1. Open ticket
2. Type response in comment box
3. Attach files if needed
4. Click **Send**
5. Agent receives notification

**Marking as Resolved:**
- When agent resolves issue, ticket status changes to "Resolved"
- You receive notification to confirm
- Click **Confirm Resolution** or **Reopen** if not fixed
- Auto-closes after 7 days if no response

### For Support Agents

**Claiming a Ticket:**
1. Navigate to **Ticket Queue**
2. View unassigned tickets
3. Click **Claim** to assign to yourself
4. Ticket status changes to "In Progress"

**Working on Tickets:**
1. Read ticket details and history
2. Research solution (knowledge base, colleagues)
3. Add internal notes (not visible to student)
4. Respond to student with solution
5. Update status appropriately

**Common Actions:**
- **Assign to Others**: Transfer to specialist
- **Change Priority**: Escalate or de-escalate
- **Add Tags**: Categorize for reporting
- **Merge Tickets**: Combine duplicates
- **Link Tickets**: Connect related issues

**Ticket Templates:**
- Pre-written responses for common issues
- Insert and customize template
- Saves time and ensures consistency

## Crisis Intervention

### 24/7 Crisis Support

**Emergency Situations:**
- Mental health crisis
- Suicidal thoughts
- Harm to self or others
- Sexual assault
- Domestic violence
- Severe distress

**How to Access:**
1. **Call Crisis Hotline**: (555) 999-HELP (available 24/7)
2. **Text CRISIS to 555-123**: Text-based crisis support
3. **In-Person**: Visit Student Health Center (24/7)
4. **Online**: Create ticket marked "URGENT - Crisis"

**Crisis Response Protocol:**
- Immediate response (within 5 minutes)
- Trained crisis counselor assigned
- Safety assessment conducted
- Emergency services contacted if needed
- Follow-up care arranged

### Safety Planning

**Creating a Safety Plan:**
1. Schedule appointment with crisis counselor
2. Identify warning signs and triggers
3. List coping strategies
4. Identify support network
5. Document emergency contacts
6. Review and update regularly

## Counseling Services

### Booking Counseling Appointments

**Individual Counseling:**
1. Navigate to **Counseling** → **Book Appointment**
2. Choose counselor (or auto-assign)
3. Select available time slot
4. Specify presenting concern (optional)
5. Choose format: In-person, Video call, Phone
6. Receive confirmation email

**First Session:**
- 50-minute intake session
- Discuss concerns and goals
- Complete assessment forms
- Develop treatment plan
- Schedule follow-up sessions

**Ongoing Counseling:**
- Typically 6-12 sessions
- Weekly or bi-weekly meetings
- Progress tracked in secure system
- Referral to external therapist if long-term care needed

### Group Counseling

**Available Groups:**
- Anxiety management
- Depression support
- Grief and loss
- LGBTQ+ support
- International student adjustment
- Substance abuse recovery
- Eating disorder support

**Joining a Group:**
1. View group schedule
2. Attend information session (required)
3. Complete screening with counselor
4. Commit to group attendance
5. Attend weekly sessions (6-10 weeks)

### Confidentiality

**What's Confidential:**
- All counseling sessions and records
- Ticket details (visible only to authorized staff)
- Health information protected by FERPA/HIPAA

**Exceptions (Required Disclosure):**
- Imminent danger to self or others
- Child abuse or neglect
- Court order
- Student provides written consent

## Analytics & Reporting

### Ticket Analytics (Staff)

**Dashboard Metrics:**
1. Navigate to **Analytics** → **Dashboard**
2. View key metrics:
   - Total tickets (today, week, month)
   - Open vs. closed ticket ratio
   - Average resolution time
   - Customer satisfaction score
   - Top ticket categories
   - Agent performance

**Visual Reports:**
- Ticket volume trends (line chart)
- Category distribution (pie chart)
- Priority breakdown (bar chart)
- SLA compliance rate (gauge)

### Custom Reports

**Creating Reports:**
1. Navigate to **Reports** → **Create Report**
2. Select report type:
   - **Ticket Summary**: Overview of tickets by criteria
   - **Agent Performance**: Individual agent metrics
   - **Category Analysis**: Trends by ticket category
   - **SLA Report**: Service level compliance
   - **Satisfaction Report**: Student feedback scores
3. Set parameters:
   - Date range
   - Filters (category, priority, agent, status)
   - Grouping (by day, week, month)
4. Generate report
5. Export as PDF, Excel, CSV

**Scheduled Reports:**
- Configure weekly/monthly reports
- Auto-email to stakeholders
- Monitor trends over time

### Student Success Dashboard

**Tracking At-Risk Students:**
1. Navigate to **Student Success** → **Dashboard**
2. View flagged students:
   - Multiple urgent tickets
   - Counseling attendance patterns
   - Academic difficulties
   - Financial hardship
3. Assign success coach for outreach
4. Develop intervention plan
5. Monitor progress

**Success Metrics:**
- Retention rates for supported students
- Academic performance improvements
- Service utilization patterns
- Satisfaction scores

## SLA Management

### Service Level Agreements

**Response Time SLAs:**

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Critical | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| High | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| Normal | 8 hours | 3 business days |
| Low | 24 hours | 5 business days |

**Escalation Rules:**
- Auto-escalate if SLA breach imminent
- Notify supervisor at 80% of SLA time
- Priority raised one level after breach

### Monitoring SLA Compliance

**SLA Dashboard:**
1. Navigate to **SLA Monitoring**
2. View metrics:
   - Current SLA compliance rate (%)
   - Tickets at risk of breach
   - Recent breaches
   - Tickets by status and time remaining
3. Take action on at-risk tickets

**Breach Notifications:**
- Email alert to agent and supervisor
- Ticket highlighted in red
- Automatic status report to management

## Best Practices

### For Students

1. **Be Specific**: Provide detailed description of issue
2. **Include Screenshots**: Helps agents understand problem
3. **Check Knowledge Base First**: May find instant solution
4. **Respond Promptly**: When agent requests information
5. **Confirm Resolution**: Close ticket when satisfied
6. **Provide Feedback**: Rate service to help improve

### For Agents

1. **Acknowledge Quickly**: Even if can't resolve immediately
2. **Set Expectations**: Provide realistic timelines
3. **Use Templates Wisely**: Customize for each student
4. **Document Thoroughly**: Add internal notes for continuity
5. **Follow Up**: Check in after resolution
6. **Escalate When Needed**: Don't hesitate to seek help
7. **Update Knowledge Base**: Share solutions for common issues

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: "Can't create ticket"**
- Ensure all required fields filled
- Check file attachment size (max 10MB)
- Clear browser cache and retry
- Try different browser

**Problem: "Not receiving email notifications"**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address in profile
- Check notification preferences
- Whitelist `support@university.edu`

**Problem: "Can't view ticket details"**
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check that ticket belongs to you
- Refresh page
- Contact support if issue persists
