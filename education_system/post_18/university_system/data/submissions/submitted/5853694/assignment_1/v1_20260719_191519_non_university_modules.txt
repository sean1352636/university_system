NOTES
================================================================================
* Most retail shops exist in BOTH layers: a full module under domain/commerce/
  (GUI + CLI) and a launcher entry point under services/cli/.
* Food-service (grocery, takeaway, restaurant) is GUI-only; bar and cafe have
  both a GUI and a services/cli entry.
* Event-bus wiring for these lives in services/commerce_bus.py,
  restaurant_bus.py and loyalty_bus.py (no direct user interface).
* Borderline (retail but university-adjacent, included for completeness):
  marketplace, textbooks, integration_marketplace, health_portal.
* Genuine university functions that happen to sit in services/cli/ and are
  NOT listed above: academic_misconduct_cli, legal_services_cli,
  medical_accommodation_cli, equipment_rental_cli, security_desk_cli, todo_cli.
