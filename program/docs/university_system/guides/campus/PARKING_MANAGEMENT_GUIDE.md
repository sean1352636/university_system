## Getting Started

### Accessing Parking Management

**For Permit Holders:**
1. Navigate to **Services** → **Parking Management**
2. Login with university credentials
3. Manage permits, view violations, make payments

**For Visitors:**
1. Visit `parking.university.edu`
2. Click **Visitor Parking**
3. Purchase day pass or pay for parking

### Understanding Parking Zones

**Campus Parking Zones:**

| Zone | Color | Who Can Park | Cost/Year | Availability |
|------|-------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| **A** | Gold | Faculty, Staff (senior) | $600 | High demand, limited |
| **B** | Blue | Faculty, Staff | $450 | Moderate availability |
| **C** | Green | Students (senior/grad) | $350 | Available |
| **D** | Orange | Students (undergrad) | $250 | Available |
| **V** | Purple | Visitors (daily) | $10/day | Pay-per-use |
| **E** | Yellow | Electric Vehicles | Varies | Charging stations |
| **R** | Red | Reserved/Handicap | Varies | By permit only |

**Zone Locations:**
- Interactive campus map showing all zones
- Click zone to view available lots
- Real-time availability displayed

## Parking Permits

### Applying for a Permit

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **Permits** → **Apply for Permit**
2. Select permit type based on your status:
   - **Faculty/Staff**: Zone A or B
   - **Graduate Student**: Zone C
   - **Undergraduate Student**: Zone D
   - **Commuter**: Special commuter rates
   - **Motorcycle**: Discounted rate
   - **Carpool**: 2+ registered carpoolers
3. Provide vehicle information:
   - Make, model, color
   - License plate number
   - State/province of registration
   - VIN (optional, for verification)
4. Upload insurance card (required)
5. Select billing preference:
   - **One-time payment**: Full amount upfront
   - **Monthly installments**: Auto-debit
   - **Payroll deduction**: Faculty/staff only
6. Review and submit application

**Approval Process:**
- Automatic approval for standard permits
- Receive virtual permit via email (24-48 hours)
- Physical hang tag mailed if requested (5-7 days)

### Permit Types & Pricing

**Annual Permits (Academic Year: Aug-May):**
- Zone A: $600
- Zone B: $450
- Zone C: $350
- Zone D: $250
- Motorcycle: $125
- Carpool (2+ people): 20% discount

**Short-Term Permits:**
- **Semester**: 60% of annual cost
- **Summer**: $150 (all zones)
- **Monthly**: 15% of annual cost

**Special Permits:**
- **Handicap**: Free (requires state-issued placard)
- **Electric Vehicle**: Zone E, includes charging access
- **Reserved Space**: $1,200/year (limited availability)

### Managing Your Permit

**View Permit Details:**
1. Navigate to **Permits** → **My Permits**
2. View active permits:
   - Permit number and zone
   - Vehicle registered
   - Expiration date
   - Virtual permit (QR code)

**Actions:**
- **Download Virtual Permit**: Save to phone for display
- **Print Hang Tag**: Print at home if you have physical permit
- **Update Vehicle**: Change license plate or vehicle
- **Add Vehicle**: Register additional vehicle (max 2)
- **Renew Early**: Renew before expiration
- **Transfer**: Switch to different zone (fee applies)

**Adding/Changing Vehicles:**
1. Click **Update Vehicle**
2. Enter new license plate
3. System allows up to 2 registered vehicles
4. Only one can park on campus at a time
5. Changes effective immediately

### Renewing Permits

**Renewal Reminders:**
- Email 60, 30, and 7 days before expiration
- SMS alert (if opted in)
- Auto-renewal available

**Renewal Process:**
1. Receive renewal notice
2. Click **Renew** in permit management
3. Verify vehicle information
4. Confirm payment method
5. Submit renewal
6. Receive new permit immediately

**Auto-Renewal:**
- Enable in permit settings
- Auto-charge saved payment method
- Receive confirmation email
- Can cancel anytime before renewal date

## Visitor Parking

### Daily Visitor Passes

**For Campus Visitors:**
1. Visit `parking.university.edu/visitor`
2. Select:
   - **Date**: Today or future date
   - **Duration**: Hourly ($3/hr) or Daily ($10/day)
   - **Lot**: Choose from visitor lots (Zone V)
3. Enter vehicle information:
   - License plate
   - Vehicle description
4. Make payment (credit card)
5. Receive virtual pass via email/SMS
6. Display confirmation on dashboard or enter license plate

**Physical Ticket Option:**
- Available at parking kiosks in visitor lots
- Pay by credit card or mobile payment
- Display paper ticket on dashboard

### Hosting Visitors

**Faculty/Staff Hosting Visitors:**
1. Navigate to **Visitor Parking** → **Issue Visitor Pass**
2. Enter visitor details:
   - Name, organization
   - License plate
   - Date and estimated duration
3. Select lot (typically near your building)
4. Submit (free for official university business)
5. Visitor receives pass via email

**Restrictions:**
- Maximum 5 visitor passes per month
- Each pass valid up to 3 consecutive days
- Abuse of system may result in suspension

## Violations & Appeals

### Common Violations

| Violation | Fine | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **No Valid Permit** | $50 | Parking without permit or expired permit |
| **Wrong Zone** | $40 | Valid permit but parked in wrong zone |
| **Handicap Violation** | $250 | Parking in handicap spot without placard |
| **Fire Lane** | $100 | Parking in fire lane or blocking access |
| **Reserved Space** | $75 | Parking in reserved/assigned space |
| **Overtime** | $25 | Exceeding time limit (visitor lots) |
| **Double Parking** | $50 | Blocking another vehicle |

### Receiving a Violation

**How You're Notified:**
1. Physical citation placed on windshield
2. Email notification (within 24 hours)
3. SMS alert (if opted in)

**Citation Details:**
- Violation code and description
- Date, time, location
- Fine amount
- Due date (typically 14 days)
- Payment instructions
- Appeal process

### Paying a Citation

**Payment Methods:**
1. **Online** (recommended):
   - Navigate to **Violations** → **Pay Citation**
   - Enter citation number
   - Pay with credit/debit card
2. **In Person**:
   - Parking Services office
   - Cash, check, or card accepted
3. **Mail**:
   - Check payable to University Parking
   - Include citation number

**Late Fees:**
- 14 days: Original fine
- 15-30 days: +$10 late fee
- 30+ days: +$25 late fee, hold on account (can't register or get transcript)

### Appealing a Citation

**Grounds for Appeal:**
- Medical emergency
- Vehicle breakdown
- Incorrect citation (wrong plate, location)
- Parking signs unclear or missing
- Permit not properly displayed but valid
- University business (with supervisor verification)

**Appeal Process:**
1. Navigate to **Violations** → **Appeal Citation**
2. Select citation to appeal
3. Choose appeal reason
4. Provide detailed explanation
5. Upload supporting evidence:
   - Photos of signage or vehicle
   - Medical documentation
   - Repair receipts
   - Supervisor letter
6. Submit appeal (within 14 days of citation)

**Appeal Review:**
- Parking Appeals Board meets weekly
- Decision typically within 7-10 business days
- Email notification of decision
- Options:
  - **Approved**: Fine waived
  - **Reduced**: Fine reduced (partial payment)
  - **Denied**: Full fine due

**Second Appeal:**
- If denied, can request second review
- Submit within 7 days of first decision
- Final decision by Director of Parking Services

## Electric Vehicle Charging

### EV Charging Stations

**Locations:**
- 15 charging stations across campus (Zone E lots)
- Level 2 chargers (3-4 hour full charge)
- 2 DC fast chargers (30-45 minute charge)

**Finding Chargers:**
1. Navigate to **EV Charging**
2. View map of charging stations
3. Real-time availability:
   - Green: Available
   - Red: In use
   - Yellow: Reserved

### Using Charging Stations

**Access:**
- Requires EV permit (Zone E)
- Or pay-per-use via mobile app

**Charging Process:**
1. Park in EV charging space
2. Plug in vehicle
3. Authenticate via:
   - RFID card (provided with EV permit)
   - Mobile app
   - Credit card at station
4. Charging starts automatically
5. Receive notification when charging complete
6. Move vehicle promptly (idle fees apply)

**Pricing:**
- **EV Permit Holders**: Free (included in $450 Zone E permit)
- **Pay-Per-Use**: $2.00 + $0.25/kWh
- **DC Fast Charging**: $0.40/kWh

**Idle Fees:**
- $1.00 per 15 minutes after charging complete
- Encourages turnover
- Notification sent 10 minutes before charge completes

## Enforcement

### For Parking Enforcement Officers

**Enforcement Schedule:**
- Monday-Friday: 7 AM - 7 PM
- Weekends: 9 AM - 5 PM (events may extend)
- Holidays: Limited enforcement

**Issuing Citations:**
1. Scan license plate (LPR system)
2. System checks for valid permit
3. If violation, create citation:
   - Select violation type
   - Take photo of vehicle and violation
   - Attach citation to windshield
   - Email sent automatically
4. Upload to system

**License Plate Recognition (LPR):**
- Mobile LPR units scan plates while patrolling
- Automatically identifies vehicles without permits
- Alerts officer to scofflaws (multiple unpaid citations)

### Parking Enforcement Policies

**Grace Periods:**
- New students: 1-week grace period (start of term)
- Expired permits: 48-hour grace period

**Repeat Offenders:**
- 3+ unpaid citations: Vehicle towed at owner's expense
- Account hold preventing registration/graduation

**Towing:**
- Handicap violations: Immediate tow
- Fire lane: Immediate tow
- 3+ unpaid citations: Tow after notification
- Towing fee: $150 + storage ($25/day)

## Best Practices

### For Permit Holders

1. **Display Permit Properly**: Hang tag visible from windshield, virtual permit readily accessible
2. **Park in Correct Zone**: Double-check zone color and signs
3. **Renew Early**: Don't let permit lapse
4. **Update Vehicle Info**: Keep license plate current
5. **Move Promptly**: From EV chargers when done
6. **Appeal Quickly**: Don't wait until deadline

### For Enforcement Officers

1. **Document Thoroughly**: Clear photos of violation
2. **Be Consistent**: Apply rules fairly
3. **Verify Information**: Double-check plate entry
4. **Note Extenuating Circumstances**: In system for appeals
5. **Respond to Appeals**: Provide additional information if requested

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: "Permit not recognized by LPR"**
- Verify license plate in system matches vehicle
- Check for typos in plate entry
- Update if plate changed
- Contact Parking Services

**Issue: "Cannot renew permit"**
- Outstanding citations must be paid first
- Payment method may be expired
- Contact Parking Services

**Issue: "EV charger not working"**
- Try different charging port (stations have 2)
- Ensure RFID card properly scanned
- Report issue via app
- Use alternative charger
