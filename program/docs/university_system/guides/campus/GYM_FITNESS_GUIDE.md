# Gym & Fitness Center - User Guide

## Overview

The Gym & Fitness Center system manages memberships, fitness class bookings, equipment tracking, and personal training sessions. It offers tiered membership plans for students and staff, a diverse class schedule, and integrated equipment maintenance tracking to ensure a safe and effective fitness environment.

## Getting Started

| Plan | Monthly Fee | Includes |
|------|------------|----------|

| **Basic** | $25/month | Gym access |
| **Standard** | $40/month | Gym access, all classes, pool |

| Cardio Equipment | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Weight Room | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Fitness Classes | Basic | No | All | All | All |
| Swimming Pool | No | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Sauna & Steam | No | No | No | Yes | No |

### Enrolling in a Membership

**Sign Up:**
1. Navigate to **Gym** → **Memberships** → **Enroll**
2. Select your membership plan
3. Review features and pricing
4. Complete health waiver form
5. Set up payment method
6. Confirm enrollment
7. Receive membership ID (format: GYM-YYYYMM-XXXXXX)

### Managing Your Membership

**Membership Actions:**
- **View Status**: Check active/inactive status and renewal date
- **Upgrade/Downgrade**: Change plan (effective next billing cycle)
- **Pause**: Freeze membership for up to 3 months (medical/travel)
- **Cancel**: End membership (30-day notice required)
- **Auto-Renewal**: Enable or disable automatic renewal

**Renewal:**
- Memberships renew monthly on the enrollment date
- Auto-renewal is enabled by default
- Receive reminder email 7 days before renewal
- Payment charged to your registered method

### Booking a Class

**Reserve Your Spot:**
1. Navigate to **Gym** → **Fitness Classes**
2. Browse the weekly schedule
3. Filter by:
   - Class type
   - Day of the week
   - Time of day
   - Instructor
   - Difficulty level
4. Select a class
5. View details:
   - Instructor name
   - Date and time
   - Location/studio
   - Available spots
6. Click **Book Class**
7. Receive confirmation

### Class Policies

**Attendance:**
- Arrive 5-10 minutes before class starts
- Bring water bottle and towel
- Wear appropriate athletic attire
- Late arrivals (10+ minutes) may not be admitted

**Cancellation:**
- Cancel at least 2 hours before class
- Late cancellations count as a no-show
- 3 no-shows in a month: 2-week booking restriction

**Waitlist:**
- Join the waitlist if class is full
- Notified if a spot opens
- Must confirm within 30 minutes

### Booking a Personal Trainer

**Schedule a Session:**
1. Navigate to **Gym** → **Personal Training**
2. Browse available trainers:
   - Trainer name and photo
   - Specialties (weight loss, strength, sports, rehabilitation)
   - Certifications
   - Availability
3. Select a trainer
4. Choose a package
5. Book your first session:
   - Select date and time
   - Specify goals and any injuries
6. Confirm booking and payment

### What to Expect

**First Session (Assessment):**
- Fitness assessment (strength, flexibility, cardio)
- Body measurements (optional)
- Discuss goals and limitations
- Create personalized workout plan
- Duration: 60 minutes

**Ongoing Sessions:**
- Guided workouts with your trainer
- Form correction and technique coaching
- Progress tracking
- Plan adjustments based on progress
- Duration: 45-60 minutes

## Best Practices

1. **Start with an orientation** - Tour the facility and learn proper equipment use
2. **Book classes in advance** - Popular classes fill quickly
3. **Warm up and cool down** - Prevent injuries with proper preparation
4. **Wipe down equipment** - Clean machines after each use
5. **Stay hydrated** - Bring a water bottle to every workout
6. **Report broken equipment** - Help maintain a safe environment
7. **Respect time limits** - During peak hours, limit cardio machines to 30 minutes

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot book a class:**
- Ensure your membership plan includes classes
- Check that the class is not already full
- Verify you don't have booking restrictions (no-show policy)
- Student plan only includes basic classes

**Membership payment failed:**
- Check that payment method is valid and not expired
- Ensure sufficient funds available
- Update payment information in account settings
- Contact front desk for manual processing

**Equipment out of order:**
- Report to front desk or through the app
- Check the equipment status board
- Use alternative equipment for similar exercises
- Maintenance team responds within 24 hours

**Cannot access pool/sauna:**
- Pool access requires Standard or Premium membership
- Sauna requires Premium membership
- Consider upgrading your plan
- Check facility hours (may differ from gym hours)
