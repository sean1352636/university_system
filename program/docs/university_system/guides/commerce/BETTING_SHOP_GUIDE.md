It features account funding, sports event wagering, accumulator bets, slot machines, and comprehensive transaction tracking with responsible gambling controls.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Betting Shop

**GUI Mode:**
1. Launch the application → **Campus Services** → **Betting Shop**
2. Login with university credentials
3. System opens multi-tab interface

**CLI Mode:**
1. Navigate to **Main Menu** → **Betting Shop**
2. Select from the available options

### First-Time Setup

1. Log in with your university credentials
2. Your betting account is created automatically
3. Fund your account using one of the available methods
4. Review the responsible gambling information
5. Start browsing events or games

## Account Management

### Funding Your Account

1. Navigate to **Account** → **Deposit Funds**
2. Select a deposit method:
   - **Card** - Debit/credit card payment
   - **Student Account** - Transfer from university finance account
   - **Cash** - Deposit at the counter
3. Enter the amount (minimum and maximum limits apply)
4. Confirm the deposit
5. Balance updates immediately

### Checking Your Balance

Your current balance is displayed at the top of the interface and updates in real-time as you place bets, win, or make deposits.

### Withdrawing Funds

1. Navigate to **Account** → **Withdraw**
2. Select withdrawal method
3. Enter the amount
4. Confirm the withdrawal
5. Funds returned to your selected method

### Account Information

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Balance** | Current available funds (GBP) |
| **Total Wagered** | Lifetime amount bet |
| **Total Won** | Lifetime winnings |
| **Net Profit/Loss** | Overall P&L |

## Sports Betting

### Viewing Events

1. Navigate to **Sports Betting** → **View Events**
2. Browse upcoming sports events showing:
   - Event name and sport type
   - Teams (Team A vs Team B)
   - Odds for each outcome (Team A win, Team B win, Draw)
   - Event date and time

### Placing a Single Bet

1. Select **Place Sports Bet**
2. Choose an event from the available list
3. Select bet type:
   - **Single Bet (Win)** - Standard win bet
   - **Each-Way Bet** - Win or place (doubles the stake)
4. Choose your selection:
   - Team A (with odds)
   - Team B (with odds)
   - Draw (with odds, if available)
5. Enter your stake amount
6. Review the bet summary:
   - Event and selection
   - Bet type and stake
   - Odds and potential return
   - Potential profit
7. Confirm with "yes" to place the bet

### Placing an Accumulator Bet

An accumulator combines multiple selections where all must win for a payout. Odds multiply together for higher potential returns.

1. Select **Place Accumulator Bet**
2. Add selections one at a time:
   - Choose event → Choose team/outcome
   - Combined odds update as you add selections
3. Minimum 2 selections required
4. Enter your stake amount
5. Review combined odds and potential return
6. Confirm to place the accumulator

### Viewing Your Bets

1. Select **View My Bets**
2. Filter by status:
   - **All Bets** - Complete history
   - **Pending Bets** - Awaiting results
   - **Won Bets** - Successful bets
   - **Lost Bets** - Unsuccessful bets
3. Each bet shows:
   - Bet ID, event, type, selection
   - Stake, odds, potential/actual return
   - Status with visual indicators
4. Summary at bottom shows total staked, returned, and net P&L

### Bet Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting event result |
| **Won** | Bet successful, winnings credited |
| **Lost** | Bet unsuccessful |
| **Cashed Out** | Early cashout taken |

### Bet Limits

| Limit | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| **Minimum Bet** | Configurable minimum stake |
| **Maximum Bet** | Configurable maximum stake |

## Responsible Gambling

### Setting Limits

The system supports responsible gambling practices:
- **Deposit Limits** - Set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit caps
- **Bet Limits** - Maximum stake per bet enforced
- **Balance Checks** - Insufficient funds prevented at bet placement

### Self-Exclusion

If you need to take a break:
- Contact the university support services
- Request temporary or permanent account suspension
- All pending bets will be settled normally

### Getting Help

If gambling is affecting your wellbeing:
- **Student Support Services**: Available on campus
- **GamCare**: Free confidential support
- **National Gambling Helpline**: 0808 8020 133

## Best Practices

1. **Set a budget** - Only bet what you can afford to lose
2. **Review odds carefully** - Understand implied probabilities
3. **Track your bets** - Use the transaction history to monitor spending
4. **Don't chase losses** - Stick to your budget regardless of results
5. **Take breaks** - Step away regularly
6. **Use accumulators wisely** - Higher potential returns come with lower probability

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot place a bet:**
- Check your account balance is sufficient
- Verify the event is still "upcoming" (not started or completed)
- Ensure the stake is within minimum/maximum limits
- Confirm you are logged in

**Deposit not showing:**
- Refresh the account page
- Check the transaction history for the deposit record
- If using student account, verify sufficient funds in finance account
- Contact support if the issue persists

**Bet result not updated:**
- Event results are updated by administrators
- Check if the event has been completed
- Pending bets settle automatically when results are entered

**Account balance incorrect:**
- Review transaction history for all recent activity
- Check for pending bets that have been deducted
- Contact support for balance discrepancies
