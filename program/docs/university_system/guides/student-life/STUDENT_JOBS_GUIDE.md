# Student Jobs Board - User Guide

## Overview

The Student Jobs Board manages on-campus employment opportunities for students, including work-study positions, part-time jobs, internships, and temporary employment. It provides job posting management, application tracking, employment records, and work hour logging with automatic earnings calculation and approval workflows.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Jobs Board

**For Students:**
1. Navigate to **Student Services** → **Student Jobs**
2. Login with university credentials
3. Browse available positions

**For Employers/Supervisors:**
1. Navigate to **Administration** → **Student Employment**
2. Post jobs and manage student employees

**Student Employment Office Hours:**
- Monday-Friday: 9 AM - 5 PM
- Email: studentjobs@university.edu
- Walk-in hours: Tuesday & Thursday 1 PM - 3 PM

### First-Time Setup

**Complete Your Employment Profile:**
1. Navigate to **Profile** → **Employment Information**
2. Fill in required details:
   - Contact information
   - Academic schedule (availability)
   - Work authorization status
   - Skills and experience
   - Resume upload
3. Submit profile

## Applying for Jobs

### Submitting an Application

**Apply for a Position:**
1. Click on the job posting
2. Click **Apply Now**
3. Complete application:
   - **Cover Letter**: Write or paste your cover letter
   - **Resume**: Select from uploaded resumes or upload new
   - **Availability**: Specify your weekly availability
   - **References**: Provide reference contact information
4. Review application
5. Submit

### Application Status Tracking

**Status Workflow:**

```
Pending → Reviewed → Interview → Offered → Accepted
                              ↘ Rejected
```

**Status Definitions:**
- **Pending**: Application submitted, awaiting review
- **Reviewed**: Supervisor has reviewed your application
- **Interview**: Selected for an interview
- **Offered**: Job offer extended
- **Accepted**: You accepted the offer
- **Rejected**: Application not selected

### Managing Applications

**My Applications:**
1. Navigate to **Jobs** → **My Applications**
2. View all submitted applications:
   - Position title and department
   - Application date
   - Current status
   - Interview date (if scheduled)
3. Actions:
   - **Withdraw**: Cancel your application
   - **Update**: Modify cover letter or resume (if still pending)
   - **View Details**: See full application and status history

## Employment Management

### Active Employment

**Once Hired:**
1. Navigate to **Employment** → **My Employment**
2. View your employment record:
   - Job title and department
   - Supervisor name
   - Employment status (active, on-leave, completed, terminated)
   - Start date
   - Hourly rate
   - Work-study allocation (if applicable)

### Employment Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active** | Currently employed |
| **On Leave** | Temporary leave of absence |
| **Completed** | Employment term finished successfully |
| **Terminated** | Employment ended early |

### Work-Study Employment

**Work-Study Specifics:**

## Work Hour Tracking

### Logging Work Hours

**Clock In/Out:**
1. Navigate to **Employment** → **Time Tracking**
2. Click **Clock In** at the start of your shift
3. Work your shift
4. Click **Clock Out** at the end of your shift
5. System records:
   - Clock-in timestamp
   - Clock-out timestamp
   - Break duration (enter if applicable)
   - Task description (brief summary of work done)
   - Automatic hours calculation
   - Automatic earnings calculation

### Manual Time Entry

**Adding Hours Manually:**
1. Navigate to **Employment** → **Time Tracking** → **Add Entry**
2. Enter:
   - Date
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Break duration
   - Task description
3. Submit for supervisor approval

### Viewing Work History

**Time Log:**
1. Navigate to **Employment** → **Work History**
2. View all logged hours:
   - Date and shift times
   - Hours worked
   - Break duration
   - Earnings for shift
   - Approval status
   - Task description

### Earnings Summary

**Pay Information:**
- Total hours worked (current pay period)
- Total earnings (current pay period)
- Work-study deductions (if applicable)
- Year-to-date earnings
- Payment history

## For Employers/Supervisors

### Creating Job Postings

**Post a New Position:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Student Jobs** → **Create Posting**
2. Enter position details:
   - Job title and description
   - Department
   - Employment type (work-study, part-time, internship, temporary)
   - Hourly rate
   - Hours per week
   - Required skills and preferred skills
   - Minimum GPA requirement (optional)
   - Work authorization requirement
   - Schedule flexibility
   - Application deadline
3. Publish posting

### Reviewing Applications

**Application Management:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Applications**
2. View applications for your postings
3. For each application:
   - Review resume and cover letter
   - Check student's availability
   - View references
4. Update status:
   - **Reviewed**: Mark as reviewed
   - **Interview**: Schedule interview (set date)
   - **Offered**: Extend job offer
   - **Rejected**: Decline application (optional feedback)

### Managing Student Employees

**Employee Management:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Student Employees**
2. View all student employees in your department
3. For each employee:
   - View employment details
   - Update employment status
   - Set performance ratings
   - Manage work-study allocations

### Approving Work Hours

**Hour Approval Workflow:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Time Approvals**
2. View pending time entries from your students
3. For each entry:
   - Review hours and task description
   - **Approve**: Confirm hours for payment
   - **Reject**: Deny with reason
   - **Request Correction**: Ask student to update entry
4. Approved hours move to payment processing

**Approval Status:**
- **Pending**: Awaiting supervisor review
- **Approved**: Hours confirmed, payment processing
- **Paid**: Payment completed
- **Rejected**: Hours denied by supervisor

## Best Practices

### For Students

1. **Keep your profile updated** - Maintain current availability and skills
2. **Apply early** - Competitive positions fill quickly
3. **Log hours promptly** - Enter work hours on the same day
4. **Include task descriptions** - Brief notes help with hour approval
5. **Monitor work-study balance** - Track your remaining allocation
6. **Communicate with your supervisor** - Inform them of schedule changes in advance

### For Supervisors

1. **Post clear descriptions** - Detailed job postings attract better candidates
2. **Review applications promptly** - Respond within 1-2 weeks
3. **Approve hours regularly** - Don't let time entries accumulate
4. **Provide feedback** - Help students grow professionally
5. **Monitor work-study limits** - Ensure students don't exceed allocations

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot apply for a position:**
- Verify your employment profile is complete
- Check that the application deadline hasn't passed
- Ensure you meet the minimum GPA requirement
- Verify work authorization status is current

**Hours not showing in time log:**
- Ensure you clocked out properly
- Manual entries require supervisor approval
- Check the date range filter on the time log
- Contact your supervisor if entries are missing

**Work-study allocation exceeded:**
- System prevents logging hours beyond your allocation
- Contact the Financial Aid office to verify your award
- Discuss with your supervisor about non-work-study options

**Payment not received:**
- Verify hours have been approved by supervisor
- Check payroll processing schedule (typically biweekly)
- Ensure direct deposit information is correct
- Contact Student Employment for payment inquiries

**Application status not changing:**
- Supervisors review on their own timeline
- Contact the department if no response after 2 weeks
- Check for email notifications about status updates
- Verify the position is still open
