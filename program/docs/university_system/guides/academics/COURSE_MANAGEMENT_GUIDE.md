## Getting Started

### Accessing Course Management

**For Students:**
1. Navigate to **Academics** → **Course Management**
2. View available courses and registration options

**For Instructors:**
1. Navigate to **Academics** → **My Courses**
2. Manage course rosters and materials

**For Administrators:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Course Management**
2. Full access to course catalog and enrollment management

### User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Student** | Browse catalog, register for courses, view grades |
| **Instructor** | Manage course content, view roster, submit grades |
| **Academic Advisor** | View student transcripts, advise on course selection |
| **Department Chair** | Manage department courses, assign instructors |
| **Registrar** | Full catalog management, enrollment overrides |

## Course Catalog

### Browsing the Course Catalog

**Basic Search:**
1. Navigate to **Course Catalog**
2. View all available courses for current term
3. Use quick filters:
   - **Department/Subject**: CS, MATH, ENG, etc.
   - **Level**: 100, 200, 300, 400, Graduate
   - **Credits**: 1-4+ credits
   - **Term**: Fall, Spring, Summer

**Advanced Search:**
1. Click **Advanced Search**
2. Apply multiple filters:
   - **Days**: MWF, TR, weekend
   - **Time Range**: Morning, afternoon, evening
   - **Instructor**: Search by name
   - **Location**: Building/room
   - **Seats Available**: Only show open courses
   - **Attributes**: Honors, Writing Intensive, Online, Hybrid
   - **General Education**: Fulfills gen ed requirements

**Course Details Page:**
Click any course to view:
- **Course Information**:
  - Code, title, credits
  - Description and learning outcomes
  - Prerequisites and corequisites
  - Textbook requirements
- **Offering Details**:
  - Instructor(s)
  - Meeting times and location
  - Enrollment stats (current/max)
  - Waitlist status
- **Historical Data**:
  - Past grade distributions
  - Average class size
  - Instructor ratings
  - Typical enrollment patterns

### Course Comparison

**Comparing Courses:**
1. Browse catalog and check boxes next to courses
2. Click **Compare Selected** (max 4 courses)
3. View side-by-side comparison:
   - Schedule compatibility
   - Workload (credits, assignments)
   - Instructor ratings
   - Prerequisites met
   - Degree requirements fulfilled
4. Add to cart directly from comparison

## Course Registration

### Registration Periods

**Priority Registration:**
- Based on total earned credits (seniors first)
- Athletes, honors students may get early access
- View your registration time: **My Profile** → **Registration Time**

**Registration Phases:**
1. **Early Registration**: Priority students
2. **General Registration**: All students
3. **Late Registration**: After term starts (fee applies)
4. **Add/Drop Period**: First 2 weeks of term
5. **Withdrawal Period**: Up to week 10 (W grade)

### Adding Courses

**Via Course Search:**
1. Search for course in catalog
2. Click **Add to Cart**
3. Repeat for other courses
4. Navigate to **Shopping Cart**
5. Review schedule (check for conflicts)
6. Click **Enroll** to register

**Direct Entry (if you know course code):**
1. Navigate to **Quick Add**
2. Enter course code (e.g., CS101-001)
3. Click **Add**
4. Confirm enrollment

**Enrollment Validation:**
- System checks prerequisites automatically
- Alerts if time conflict exists
- Warns if course full (option to join waitlist)
- Flags if you exceed credit limit (overload requires approval)

### Dropping Courses

**During Add/Drop Period:**
1. Navigate to **My Schedule**
2. Find course to drop
3. Click **Drop**
4. Confirm (no penalty, 100% refund)

**After Add/Drop (Withdrawal):**
1. Navigate to **My Schedule**
2. Click **Withdraw** next to course
3. Fill withdrawal form (reason required)
4. Submit for advisor approval
5. If approved, receive "W" grade (affects GPA after deadline)

**Refund Schedule:**
- Week 1-2: 100% refund
- Week 3-4: 50% refund
- After Week 4: No refund

## Waitlist Management

### Joining a Waitlist

**When a Course is Full:**
1. Click **Join Waitlist** on course page
2. Confirm enrollment in waitlist
3. Receive confirmation with waitlist position
4. Get notified when seat becomes available

**Waitlist Rules:**
- Maximum 2 waitlists per student
- Must meet prerequisites to join waitlist
- Waitlist position based on timestamp (first come, first served)
- Special exceptions: Graduating seniors, major requirements

### Waitlist Notifications

**When Seat Opens:**
1. Receive email and SMS notification
2. **24-hour window to accept**
3. Log in and click **Accept Enrollment**
4. If not accepted, offer goes to next person

**Notification Preferences:**
1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Enable email and/or SMS for waitlists
3. Provide mobile number for SMS

### For Instructors: Managing Waitlists

**Viewing Waitlist:**
1. Navigate to **My Courses** → Select course
2. Click **Waitlist** tab
3. View student names and waitlist positions

**Adding Students from Waitlist:**
1. If student drops or you increase capacity
2. System auto-enrolls top student on waitlist
3. Manual override available for special cases

**Increasing Course Capacity:**
1. Request capacity increase from department
2. Department approves and updates max enrollment
3. Waitlisted students auto-enrolled in order

## Prerequisites & Requirements

### Understanding Prerequisites

**Prerequisite Types:**
- **Single Course**: Must complete CS101 before CS102
- **Multiple Options**: CS201 OR CS202
- **Grade Requirement**: CS101 with grade of C or better
- **Corequisite**: Must take MATH101 same term as CS101
- **Permission Required**: Instructor or advisor approval

**Checking Prerequisites:**
1. View course details page
2. Prerequisites listed clearly
3. System automatically validates when registering
4. Green checkmark = met, Red X = not met

### Prerequisite Overrides

**Requesting Override:**
1. If you believe you meet prerequisites via transfer credit, experience, etc.
2. Navigate to course page
3. Click **Request Override**
4. Fill override form:
   - Reason for request
   - Supporting evidence (transcript, portfolio)
5. Submit to instructor or department
6. Await approval (typically 2-3 business days)

**Override Approval:**
- Instructor reviews request
- Can approve or deny with reason
- If approved, you can register for course
- Override valid for current term only

## Instructor Management

### For Instructors: Managing Your Courses

**Viewing Course Roster:**
1. Navigate to **My Courses**
2. Select course
3. View student list with:
   - Name, student ID, major, year
   - Email and contact information
   - Enrollment status (enrolled, waitlist, dropped)
   - Photo (if available)

**Roster Actions:**
- **Export Roster**: Download as CSV or PDF
- **Email Class**: Send announcement to all students
- **Print Roster**: For attendance or records
- **Filter**: By year, major, enrollment status

**Managing Enrollment:**
- **Add Student**: Override enrollment (requires permission)
- **Drop Student**: Remove for non-attendance (requires justification)
- **View Attendance**: Track who's attending class
- **Permissions**: Grant course access (late adds, auditors)

**Course Materials:**
- Upload syllabus, assignments, readings
- Integration with Assignment System
- Share resources via LMS

### For Administrators: Assigning Instructors

**Creating Course Sections:**
1. Navigate to **Admin** → **Course Catalog**
2. Select course
3. Click **Add Section**
4. Fill section details:
   - Section number
   - Meeting times (days, start/end time)
   - Location (building, room)
   - Max enrollment
   - Term
5. Assign instructor(s):
   - Primary instructor
   - Co-instructors or TAs
6. Set course attributes (online, hybrid, honors)
7. Publish section

**Instructor Assignment:**
- View instructor availability and load
- Check for scheduling conflicts
- Consider instructor preferences and expertise
- Ensure balanced teaching assignments

## Course Evaluations

### For Students: Evaluating Courses

**When Evaluations Open:**
- Last 2 weeks of term
- Receive email reminder
- Must complete before viewing final grades (configurable)

**Completing Evaluation:**
1. Navigate to **Course Evaluations**
2. View list of enrolled courses
3. Click **Start Evaluation** for each course
4. Rate on 5-point scale:
   - Overall course quality
   - Instructor effectiveness
   - Course organization
   - Workload appropriateness
   - Learning outcomes achieved
5. Provide written comments (optional but encouraged):
   - What worked well
   - Suggestions for improvement
   - Instructor strengths
6. Submit (anonymous)

**Incentives:**
- Early submission: Entered in raffle for prizes
- Completion required to view grades
- Helps improve future courses

### For Instructors: Viewing Evaluations

**Accessing Results:**
1. Available after grades submitted and term ended
2. Navigate to **My Courses** → Select course → **Evaluations**
3. View aggregate results:
   - Average ratings per question
   - Response rate
   - Anonymous written comments
   - Comparison to department average

**Using Feedback:**
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Adjust teaching methods
- Update course materials
- Discuss with department chair for mentoring

**Evaluation Privacy:**
- Individual student responses anonymous
- Results visible to instructor, department chair, dean
- May be used in promotion/tenure decisions
- Summary stats may be public (varies by institution)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: "Cannot register - prerequisite not met"**
- Verify you completed prerequisite with required grade
- Check if transfer credit needs to be applied
- Request prerequisite override if you have equivalent experience

**Problem: "Time conflict detected"**
- Adjust schedule to avoid overlapping classes
- Look for alternate section of one course
- Contact department if both courses required

**Problem: "Credit limit exceeded"**
- Standard limit is 18 credits per term
- Request overload approval from advisor
- Provide justification (graduation requirement, good academic standing)

**Problem: "Course not appearing in catalog"**
- Ensure correct term selected
- Check if course offered this term
- Verify you have permission to view (some courses restricted)

**Problem: "Waitlist position not moving"**
- Few students may be dropping
- Check if instructor increased capacity
- Consider alternate course as backup
