## Room Bookings

### Types of Spaces Available

**Academic Spaces:**
- **Classrooms**: Lecture halls, seminar rooms (20-200 capacity)
- **Labs**: Computer labs, science labs
- **Study Rooms**: Group study (4-10 people)
- **Conference Rooms**: Departmental meetings (8-20 people)

**Event Spaces:**
- **Auditoriums**: Large gatherings (200-500)
- **Ballrooms**: Formal events (100-300)
- **Multi-Purpose Rooms**: Flexible use (50-150)
- **Outdoor Spaces**: Courtyards, amphitheaters

**Recreation Spaces:**
- **Gyms**: Sports and fitness
- **Fields**: Outdoor sports
- **Performance Spaces**: Theater, music halls

### Searching for Available Rooms

**Room Finder:**
1. Navigate to **Facilities** → **Book a Room**
2. Enter search criteria:
   - **Date & Time**: Select date and time range
   - **Duration**: How long you need space
   - **Capacity**: Number of attendees
   - **Location**: Building or zone preference
   - **Features**: AV equipment, whiteboard, accessibility
   - **Room Type**: Classroom, conference, auditorium, etc.
3. Click **Search**
4. View results showing available rooms

**Search Results Display:**
- Room name and building
- Capacity and features
- Photo of space
- Availability calendar
- Hourly rate (if applicable)
- Booking restrictions

### Booking a Room

**Standard Booking Process:**
1. Select room from search results
2. Click **Book This Room**
3. Fill booking form:
   - **Purpose**: Meeting, class, event, study group
   - **Organization**: Department, club, or personal
   - **Expected Attendees**: Number of people
   - **Setup Requirements**: Table arrangement, AV needs
   - **Catering**: Food/beverage requests
   - **Special Needs**: Accessibility, interpretation
4. Review booking policy and terms
5. Submit booking request

**Booking Approval:**
- **Auto-Approved**: Small rooms (<20 people), standard hours
- **Manual Approval**: Large rooms, after-hours, special events
- Approval typically within 24 hours
- Email notification of approval or denial

**Booking Restrictions:**
- **Academic Priority**: Classes have first priority
- **Advance Booking**: 2 weeks max for general users, 8 weeks for events
- **Duration Limits**: Max 4 hours for study rooms, no limit for events
- **Recurring Bookings**: Request through department, semester-long

### Managing Your Bookings

**View Bookings:**
1. Navigate to **My Bookings**
2. See upcoming and past bookings
3. Booking details:
   - Room, date, time
   - Status (Pending, Approved, Completed, Cancelled)
   - Setup requests
   - Confirmation number

**Modify Booking:**
1. Click booking to view details
2. Click **Modify**
3. Change date, time, or setup (subject to availability)
4. Re-submit for approval
5. Original booking held until modification approved

**Cancel Booking:**
1. Click **Cancel** next to booking
2. Confirm cancellation
3. Cancellation policy:
   - **24+ hours notice**: Full refund (if paid)
   - **Less than 24 hours**: 50% fee
   - **No-show**: Full charge

### Room Features & Equipment

**Standard Features (most rooms):**
- Tables and chairs
- Whiteboard/chalkboard
- Power outlets
- Wi-Fi access
- Climate control

**Technology-Enhanced Rooms:**
- **AV Equipment**:
  - Projector and screen

  - Microphones (wired/wireless)
  - Document camera
- **Video Conferencing**:
  - Camera and microphone array

**Requesting Special Equipment:**

## Maintenance Requests

### Submitting a Maintenance Request

**When to Submit:**
- Broken equipment or furniture
- Lighting issues (burned out bulbs, flickering)
- Temperature problems (too hot/cold)
- Plumbing issues (leaks, clogs)
- Doors/locks not working
- Pest sightings
- Cleanliness concerns
- Safety hazards

**Submission Process:**
1. Navigate to **Facilities** → **Report Issue**
2. Select issue category:
   - **Emergency**: Safety hazard, major system failure
   - **Urgent**: Affecting operations, needs prompt attention
   - **Routine**: Non-urgent, standard maintenance
3. Provide details:
   - **Location**: Building, room number, specific area
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation of issue
   - **Impact**: How it affects occupants
   - **Photo**: Upload image of issue (helpful)
4. Submit request
5. Receive work order number

**Response Times:**

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Emergency** | 15 minutes | 2 hours |
| **Urgent** | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| **Routine** | 2 business days | 5 business days |

### Tracking Your Request

**View Request Status:**
1. Navigate to **My Requests**
2. See list of submitted requests
3. Status indicators:
   - **Received**: Logged in system
   - **Assigned**: Technician assigned
   - **In Progress**: Work underway
   - **Completed**: Issue resolved
   - **Closed**: Verified complete

**Request Details:**
- Work order number
- Submission date
- Assigned technician
- Status updates with timestamps
- Resolution notes
- Option to rate service

**Notifications:**
- Email when status changes
- SMS for emergency requests (opt-in)
- Completion notification with resolution summary

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Temperature Control:**
- **Too Hot/Cold**: Submit request with desired temperature
- Facilities adjusts thermostat or investigates HVAC
- Note: Campus-wide temperature standards (68-72°F)

**Lighting:**
- Burned out bulbs: Typically replaced within 48 hours
- Flickering lights: Electrical issue, investigated urgently

**Plumbing:**
- Leaks: Emergency response
- Clogged drains: Urgent response
- Low water pressure: Routine

**Furniture:**
- Broken chairs/tables: Replaced or repaired within week
- Request additional furniture through department

**Pest Control:**
- Single sighting: Routine treatment
- Infestation: Urgent response, may require temporary closure

## Asset Management

### What is Asset Management?

**Assets Tracked:**
- **Furniture**: Desks, chairs, filing cabinets
- **Equipment**: Projectors, computers, AV equipment
- **Appliances**: Refrigerators, microwaves, water coolers
- **Tools**: Ladders, carts, maintenance equipment
- **Vehicles**: Golf carts, trucks, maintenance vehicles

**Why Track Assets:**
- Inventory management
- Preventive maintenance scheduling
- Lifecycle planning (replacement budgeting)
- Loss prevention
- Insurance purposes

### For Facilities Staff: Managing Assets

**Adding New Asset:**
1. Navigate to **Assets** → **Add Asset**
2. Enter asset details:
   - **Asset Type**: Category (furniture, equipment, etc.)
   - **Description**: Make, model, specifications
   - **Serial Number**: For tracking
   - **Location**: Building, room
   - **Purchase Date**: When acquired
   - **Cost**: Purchase price
   - **Warranty**: Expiration date
   - **Assigned To**: Department or person
3. Attach photo and documents (receipt, manual)
4. Generate asset tag/barcode
5. Save asset

**Asset Tag:**
- Unique identifier (barcode or RFID)
- Physical tag affixed to asset
- Scan to view/update asset information

**Tracking Asset Location:**
1. Scan asset tag or search by ID
2. View current location
3. Update location if moved:
   - Enter new building/room
   - Record reason for move
   - Timestamp of move

**Maintenance History:**
- View all maintenance performed on asset
- Scheduled preventive maintenance
- Repairs and service calls
- Replacement parts used
- Downtime tracking

### Asset Lifecycle Management

**Lifecycle Stages:**
1. **Acquisition**: Purchase and receive
2. **Deployment**: Assign and install
3. **Operation**: In use, routine maintenance
4. **Maintenance**: Repairs as needed
5. **Retirement**: Decommission and dispose

**Preventive Maintenance:**
- Schedule regular maintenance (HVAC filters, elevator inspections)
- Automatic work orders generated
- Extend asset life and reduce breakdowns

**Replacement Planning:**
- Track age and condition of assets
- Identify assets nearing end of life
- Budget for replacements
- Prioritize based on criticality

## Work Orders

### For Facilities Staff: Work Order Management

**Work Order Dashboard:**
1. Navigate to **Work Orders**
2. View queue of open work orders
3. Filter by:
   - Priority (Emergency, Urgent, Routine)
   - Status (Unassigned, Assigned, In Progress, Completed)
   - Category (Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, etc.)
   - Building/location
   - Date range

**Claiming Work Order:**
1. Select unassigned work order
2. Review details and location
3. Click **Claim** to assign to yourself
4. Status changes to "Assigned"

**Working on Request:**
1. Travel to location
2. Assess issue
3. Update status to "In Progress"
4. Perform work
5. Document:
   - Work performed
   - Parts used
   - Time spent
   - Photos (before/after)
6. Update status to "Completed"
7. Add resolution notes

**If Issue Cannot be Resolved:**
- Update notes with findings
- Escalate to supervisor
- Request specialized contractor
- Communicate with requester

### Work Order Analytics

**For Supervisors:**
- **Technician Performance**:
  - Work orders completed
  - Average resolution time
  - Customer satisfaction ratings
- **Building Performance**:
  - Work orders by building
  - Recurring issues (identify patterns)
  - Maintenance costs by building
- **Category Trends**:
  - Most common issues
  - Seasonal patterns
  - Preventive maintenance effectiveness

## Space Utilization

### Tracking Room Usage

**Why Track Utilization:**
- Optimize space allocation
- Identify underutilized rooms
- Plan for new construction or renovation
- Right-size room inventory

**Utilization Metrics:**
1. Navigate to **Analytics** → **Space Utilization**
2. View metrics:
   - **Occupancy Rate**: % of time room is booked
   - **Attendance vs. Capacity**: How full rooms actually are
   - **Peak Hours**: When rooms most in demand
   - **Underutilized Spaces**: Candidates for repurposing

**Visualizations:**
- **Heatmaps**: Show usage by time of day/day of week
- **Building Maps**: Color-coded by utilization rate
- **Trend Graphs**: Usage over time (semester, year)

### Space Planning

**Reallocating Space:**
- Convert underutilized spaces
- Expand high-demand functions
- Create flexible multi-use spaces

**Example:**
- Conference room booked 15% of time → Convert to study lounge
- Classroom at 95% utilization → Add similar room nearby

## Event Setup

### Requesting Event Setup

**For Event Coordinators:**
1. Book event space (see Room Bookings)
2. Navigate to **Event Setup Request**
3. Select your booking
4. Specify setup needs:
   - **Room Layout**:
     - Theater (rows of chairs)
     - Classroom (tables and chairs in rows)
     - Banquet (round tables)
     - U-Shape or Boardroom
     - Custom (upload diagram)
   - **Furniture**:
     - Number of tables and chairs
     - Head table or stage
     - Registration table
   - **Technology**:
     - Projector and screen
     - Sound system
     - Microphones (podium, wireless)
     - Lighting (spotlights, dimming)
   - **Signage**:
     - Directional signs
     - Event banners
     - Name placards
   - **Other**:
     - Coat racks
     - Trash/recycling bins
     - Stanchions (crowd control)
5. Specify timing:
   - Setup time (hours before event)
   - Breakdown time (hours after event)
6. Submit request (minimum 3 days notice)

**Setup Approval:**
- Facilities reviews request
- Checks availability of resources
- Approves or suggests modifications
- Setup crew scheduled

**Day of Event:**
- Setup crew arrives before event
- Completes setup per specifications
- Event coordinator walks through and approves
- After event, crew returns for breakdown

### Standard Room Setups

**Theater:**
- Rows of chairs facing front
- No tables (maximizes capacity)
- Use for lectures, presentations

**Classroom:**
- Tables with chairs in rows
- Facing front
- Use for training, workshops

**Banquet:**
- Round tables (8-10 per table)
- Use for dinners, galas

**U-Shape:**
- Tables arranged in U
- Use for collaborative meetings

**Boardroom:**
- Single large table, chairs around
- Use for formal meetings

## Best Practices

### For Room Booking

1. **Book Early**: Popular rooms fill up quickly
2. **Accurate Headcount**: Reserve appropriate size room
3. **Specify Setup**: Clear instructions prevent issues
4. **Test Technology**: Arrive early to test AV equipment
5. **Cancel if Not Needed**: Free up room for others

### For Maintenance Requests

1. **Provide Details**: Clear description and photos
2. **Accurate Location**: Exact room/area helps technicians
3. **Report Promptly**: Don't wait for issues to worsen
4. **Follow Up**: Rate service to help improve
5. **Emergency vs. Routine**: Use correct priority

### For Facilities Staff

1. **Document Thoroughly**: Detailed notes help future work
2. **Communicate**: Update requesters on status
3. **Preventive Maintenance**: Proactive vs. reactive
4. **Track Parts**: Inventory management prevents delays
5. **Safety First**: Follow procedures and use PPE

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: "Cannot book room"**
- Room may require approval (not auto-book)
- Conflict with existing booking
- Booking window restrictions (too far in advance)
- Contact facilities office

**Issue: "Maintenance request not showing"**
- Check email for work order number (confirmation)
- Search "My Requests" by date submitted
- May be in spam folder
- Contact facilities if not found

**Issue: "Room setup incorrect"**
- Notify facilities immediately
- Review setup request (may have been misunderstood)
- Facilities will correct ASAP
- Provide feedback to prevent recurrence
