The Cinema Booking system manages the university's on-campus cinema, providing movie scheduling, ticket booking with seat selection, snack ordering, membership/loyalty programmes, gift cards, equipment maintenance, and accessibility features. It offers a comprehensive movie-going experience with full administrative controls.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Cinema System

**GUI Mode:**
1. Launch the application → **Campus Services** → **Cinema**
2. Login with university credentials
3. Navigate via the sidebar menu to different sections

The system is organised into several major areas:
- **Movies & Screenings** - Browse and manage film listings

## Booking Tickets

### Ticket Booking Process

1. **Select a Movie**: Browse current listings and choose your film
2. **Choose a Screening**: Pick your preferred date, time, and screen
3. **Select Seats**: Interactive seat map shows:
   - Available seats (clickable)
   - Occupied seats (greyed out)
   - Accessible seats (marked with wheelchair symbol)
   - Companion seats (marked with +)
   - Couple seats (paired seating)
4. **Choose Ticket Types**: Select for each seat:
   - Adult
   - Student (discounted)
   - Child
   - Senior
5. **Add Snacks** (optional): See [Snacks & Beverages](#snacks--beverages)
6. **Review & Pay**: Confirm booking details and process payment
7. **Receive Confirmation**: Booking reference and email receipt

### Seat Types

| Type | Symbol | Description | Price Modifier |
|------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Standard** | - | Regular seating | Base price |
| **Wheelchair** | Wheelchair icon | Wheelchair-accessible space | Varies |
| **Companion** | + | Adjacent to wheelchair seats | Varies |
| **Couple** | Heart | Paired loveseat seating | Premium |
| **Premium** | Star | Enhanced comfort seats | Premium |

### Ticket Pricing

Pricing varies by:
- Screening format (Standard, 3D, IMAX)
- Seat type (standard, premium, couple)
- Ticket category (adult, student, child, senior)
- Special promotions or membership discounts

## Snacks & Beverages

### Ordering During Booking

After selecting your seats:
1. The snacks page displays the full menu
2. Use the quantity spinners to add items (0-10 per item)
3. Running total updates automatically
4. Snacks are added to your booking total

### Standalone Snack Orders

You can also order snacks without a movie booking from the dedicated snacks menu.

### Menu Items

The snack menu includes:
- Popcorn (various sizes)
- Nachos and hot dogs
- Soft drinks and water
- Sweets and chocolate
- Combo deals (bundled items at a discount)

### Dietary Information

Each snack item includes dietary labels for allergen awareness.

## Gift Cards

### Purchasing Gift Cards

1. Navigate to **Gift Cards** → **Create Gift Card**
2. Set the gift card value
3. Enter purchaser and recipient details
4. System generates a unique gift card code
5. Gift card record created with initial balance

### Using Gift Cards

1. Navigate to **Gift Cards** → **Check Balance**
2. Enter your gift card code
3. View current balance and transaction history
4. Apply to purchases during checkout

### Managing Gift Cards (Admin)

- View all gift cards with status
- Check balances and transaction history
- Deactivate lost or expired cards

## Accessibility

### Accessible Seating

The cinema provides accessible seating options in every screen:

| Seat Type | Location | Description |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| **Wheelchair Spaces** | Front rows and back rows (ends) | Designated wheelchair positions |
| **Companion Seats** | Adjacent to wheelchair spaces | For accompanying persons |
| **Couple Seats** | Back row (centre) | Paired seating |

### Seating Layout

The accessible seating map provides a visual representation of:
- Wheelchair positions (blue, wheelchair symbol)
- Companion seats (purple, + symbol)
- Couple seats (pink, heart symbol)
- Standard seats (grey)

### Accessible Bookings

1. Navigate to **Accessibility** → **View Bookings**
2. View all bookings for accessible seating
3. Configure accessible seat layouts per screen

## Equipment & Maintenance

### Equipment Tracking

The system tracks all cinema equipment:
- Projectors, sound systems, screens
- Equipment type and screen assignment
- Hours used and maximum hours before service
- Last service date and next service due
- Current operational status

### Equipment Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Operational** | Working normally |
| **Maintenance** | Currently being serviced |
| **Out of Service** | Not available for use |

### Maintenance Alerts

The system automatically flags:
- Equipment overdue for maintenance
- Equipment exceeding maximum usage hours
- Upcoming scheduled maintenance

### Maintenance Operations (Admin)

1. Navigate to **Equipment** section
2. View equipment list with status indicators
3. **Add Equipment**: Register new equipment with specifications
4. **Log Maintenance**: Record service activities
5. **Update Hours**: Track equipment usage
6. **View Schedule**: See upcoming maintenance dates

## Best Practices

1. **Book early** - Popular screenings (new releases, weekends) sell out fast
2. **Check accessibility** - Book accessible seats in advance for guaranteed availability
3. **Join the loyalty programme** - Earn points on every visit
4. **Pre-order snacks** - Save time by adding snacks during ticket booking
5. **Arrive early** - Doors open 15 minutes before screening time
6. **Check equipment alerts** - Admins should review maintenance alerts daily

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot select a seat:**
- The seat may already be booked (shown as occupied)
- Accessible seats may require specific booking through the accessibility section
- Try selecting a different screening with more availability

**Booking not confirmed:**
- Verify payment was processed successfully
- Check your email for a confirmation receipt
- Contact the cinema desk for manual verification

**Gift card balance incorrect:**
- Review the gift card transaction history
- Ensure the correct code was entered
- Contact support for balance discrepancies

**Equipment maintenance overdue:**
- Check the equipment alerts section
- Schedule maintenance immediately for overdue items
- Contact the technical team for urgent repairs
