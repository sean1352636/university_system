# Email Receipts Quick Guide

## How It Works

The Restaurant Management System now **automatically sends email receipts** to customers for all transactions.

## When Emails Are Sent

### 1. Order Placement ✉️
**When**: After placing any order (with or without payment)
**Includes**:
- Order number
- All items ordered
- Prices and totals
- Payment status

### 2. Payment Processing ✉️
**When**: After successfully processing payment via:
- Cash
- Credit/Debit Card
- Contactless
- Meal Plan
- Student Account

**Includes**:
- Payment confirmation
- Payment method details
- Transaction ID (for cards)
- Receipt of items purchased

### 3. Refunds ✉️
**When**: After processing any refund (full or partial)
**Includes**:
- Refund confirmation
- Amount refunded
- Refund method
- Expected processing time

## What You'll See

After each transaction, the success message will show:
```
✓ Receipt email sent
```
or
```
✓ Refund confirmation email sent
```

This confirms the customer has been notified.

## Customer Email Requirements

For emails to send successfully, customers need:
- A valid email address in their customer profile, OR
- To be linked to a student account with an email

**Walk-in customers** without email addresses won't receive receipts (this is normal).

## Checking Email Status

If you need to verify an email was sent:
1. Check the console output for confirmation messages
2. Look in the email logs (if you have admin access)
3. Ask IT to check the email queue

## What Customers Receive

### Payment Receipt
- Professional formatted email
- Complete transaction details
- University Restaurant branding
- Contact information

### Refund Confirmation
- Refund details and reference
- Original order information
- Processing timeline (3-5 business days)
- Contact information for questions

## Troubleshooting

**"No email address found"** message in console?
- Customer doesn't have an email in the system
- Add email to their customer profile
- Or link them to a student account

**Customer didn't receive email?**
- Check their spam/junk folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Contact IT if emails are not sending

## Staff Tips

💡 **Always ensure customer emails are up to date** before processing orders for registered customers

💡 **For walk-in customers**: Email receipts aren't sent - offer to print a receipt if needed

💡 **Student customers**: Automatically receive emails at their university email address
