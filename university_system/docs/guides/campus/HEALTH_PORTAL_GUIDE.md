# Health Portal System - User Guide

## Overview

The Health Portal provides comprehensive healthcare services for students, including appointment scheduling, medical records, prescriptions, immunization tracking, mental health services, and telehealth. It integrates with Student Health Center and Counseling Services.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Health Portal

**For Students:**
1. Navigate to **Health Services** → **Health Portal**
2. Login with university credentials
3. First-time users: Complete health profile

**Student Health Center Hours:**
- Monday-Friday: 8 AM - 6 PM
- Saturday: 10 AM - 2 PM
- Sunday: Closed
- 24/7 Nurse Hotline: (555) 123-NURS

### First-Time Setup

**Complete Your Health Profile:**
1. Navigate to **Profile** → **Health Information**
2. Fill required information:
   - **Personal**: Name, DOB, gender, contact
   - **Emergency Contact**: Name, relationship, phone
   - **Insurance**: Provider, policy number, group number
   - **Medical History**:
     - Chronic conditions
     - Past surgeries
     - Current medications
     - Allergies (medications, food, environmental)
   - **Family History**: Major health conditions
   - **Lifestyle**: Exercise, diet, tobacco/alcohol use
3. Upload insurance card (front and back)
4. Sign consent forms (HIPAA, treatment consent)
5. Submit

**Why This Matters:**
- Faster check-in for appointments
- Providers have essential information
- Emergency situations: Staff access critical data
- Insurance billing processed correctly

## Appointments

### Scheduling an Appointment

**Online Booking:**
1. Navigate to **Appointments** → **Schedule New**
2. Select appointment type:
   - **Primary Care**: General illness, physical exams
   - **Mental Health**: Counseling, psychiatry
   - **Women's Health**: Gynecology, contraception
   - **Sports Medicine**: Athletic injuries
   - **Dermatology**: Skin conditions
   - **Nutrition**: Dietary counseling
   - **Immunizations**: Vaccines, travel medicine
   - **STI Testing**: Confidential testing
3. Choose provider (or select "First Available")
4. View available time slots
5. Select date and time
6. Describe reason for visit (brief)
7. Confirm appointment
8. Receive confirmation email with:
   - Date, time, location
   - Provider name
   - Preparation instructions (if any)
   - Calendar invite attachment

**Phone Booking:**
- Call Health Center: (555) 123-HLTH
- Same-day appointments often available
- Staff assists with scheduling

**Walk-In Hours:**
- Monday-Friday: 8-10 AM
- No appointment needed
- First-come, first-served
- For urgent but non-emergency issues

### Appointment Types & Wait Times

| Appointment Type | Typical Wait | Duration |
|------------------|--------------|----------|
| **Primary Care** | 2-3 days | 20-30 min |
| **Mental Health** | 1-2 weeks | 50 min |
| **Walk-In** | Same day, 0-60 min wait | 15-20 min |
| **Urgent Care** | Same day | 20-30 min |
| **Physical Exam** | 1-2 weeks | 30-45 min |
| **Specialty** | 1-4 weeks | 30-60 min |

### Managing Appointments

**View Upcoming Appointments:**
1. Navigate to **Appointments** → **My Appointments**
2. See list of scheduled appointments
3. Click for details

**Actions:**
- **Reschedule**: Choose new date/time (24-hour notice preferred)
- **Cancel**: Cancel appointment (at least 24 hours in advance)
- **Add to Calendar**: Download .ics file
- **Get Directions**: Map to health center

**Appointment Reminders:**
- Email: 48 hours and 24 hours before
- SMS: 24 hours before (opt-in)
- Push notification: 2 hours before (mobile app)

**Late Cancellation Policy:**
- Cancel within 24 hours: Warning
- 2+ late cancellations: Appointment privileges may be restricted
- No-shows: $25 fee

### Check-In Process

**Arriving for Appointment:**
1. Arrive 10 minutes early
2. Check in at front desk or via kiosk
3. Update insurance and contact info if changed
4. Take a seat in waiting room
5. Called by medical assistant when ready

**What to Bring:**
- Student ID card
- Insurance card
- List of current medications
- Symptom diary (if applicable)

## Medical Records

### Viewing Your Records

**Accessing Medical Records:**
1. Navigate to **Medical Records**
2. View categorized records:
   - **Visit Summaries**: Notes from appointments
   - **Lab Results**: Blood work, tests
   - **Imaging**: X-rays, scans
   - **Prescriptions**: Current and past medications
   - **Immunizations**: Vaccine history
   - **Documents**: Forms, letters

**Visit Summary Details:**
- Date of visit
- Provider name
- Chief complaint (reason for visit)
- Vital signs (BP, temp, weight, height)
- Assessment and diagnosis
- Treatment plan
- Prescriptions written
- Follow-up recommendations

### Downloading Records

**Export Medical Records:**
1. Navigate to **Medical Records** → **Export**
2. Select record types to include
3. Choose date range
4. Select format:
   - **PDF**: Readable summary
   - **CCD (Continuity of Care Document)**: Standard medical format
   - **CSV**: Data export
5. Click **Generate**
6. Download file (password protected)

**Common Uses:**
- Transfer to new doctor
- Graduate school applications
- Insurance claims
- Personal records

### Sharing Records

**Grant Access to External Providers:**
1. Navigate to **Medical Records** → **Share**
2. Enter provider information:
   - Name and practice
   - Fax or email
3. Select records to share
4. Set expiration date (1-30 days)
5. Submit request
6. Records sent within 2-3 business days

**HIPAA Authorization:**
- Required for sharing with third parties
- Electronic signature
- Can revoke access anytime

## Prescriptions

### Viewing Prescriptions

**Current Medications:**
1. Navigate to **Prescriptions**
2. View active prescriptions:
   - Medication name and dosage
   - Instructions (how often, with food, etc.)
   - Prescribing provider
   - Refills remaining
   - Expiration date
3. Past medications also visible

### Requesting Refills

**Online Refill Request:**
1. Navigate to **Prescriptions** → Select medication
2. Click **Request Refill**
3. Choose pharmacy:
   - Campus pharmacy (recommended, no cost)
   - External pharmacy (insurance/cash)
4. Submit request
5. Receive notification when ready (typically 24-48 hours)

**Automatic Refills:**
- Available for chronic medications
- Enable in prescription settings
- Auto-refill 5 days before running out
- Notification when ready for pickup

### Campus Pharmacy

**Location & Hours:**
- Located in Student Health Center
- Monday-Friday: 9 AM - 5 PM
- Saturday: 10 AM - 2 PM

**Services:**
- Fill prescriptions (same day for most)
- Over-the-counter medications
- Free birth control (with student health insurance)
- Medication counseling

**Costs:**
- **With Student Health Insurance**: Most medications free or low copay ($5-15)
- **Without Insurance**: Competitive cash prices
- **Generic vs Brand**: Generics always preferred (lower cost)

**Picking Up Medications:**
1. Receive text when ready
2. Visit pharmacy
3. Show student ID
4. Pharmacist reviews instructions
5. Ask questions about side effects, interactions

## Immunizations

### Immunization Requirements

**Required for All Students:**
- **MMR** (Measles, Mumps, Rubella): 2 doses
- **Tdap** (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis): 1 dose within 10 years
- **Meningitis** (Meningococcal): 1 dose
- **Varicella** (Chickenpox): 2 doses or proof of immunity
- **COVID-19**: Per current university policy

**Recommended:**
- Annual flu vaccine
- HPV vaccine (for those under 26)
- Hepatitis B series

**International Students:**
- TB screening (required within 30 days of arrival)
- May require additional vaccines based on country of origin

### Uploading Immunization Records

**Submitting Records:**
1. Navigate to **Immunizations** → **Upload Records**
2. Take clear photo or scan of immunization card
3. Upload file (PDF, JPG, PNG)
4. System reviews and updates records (2-3 business days)
5. Receive confirmation or request for additional info

**What to Include:**
- Vaccine name
- Date administered
- Healthcare provider or clinic name
- Lot number (if available)

### Getting Vaccines on Campus

**Scheduling Vaccination:**
1. Navigate to **Appointments** → **Immunizations**
2. Select vaccine needed
3. Choose appointment time
4. Come to appointment
5. Receive vaccine + updated records

**Vaccine Costs:**
- **With Student Health Insurance**: Most vaccines covered (free)
- **Without Insurance**: Varies ($20-200 depending on vaccine)
- **Flu Shots**: Always free during flu season

**Flu Shot Clinics:**
- Held throughout fall semester
- Walk-in, no appointment needed
- Locations across campus
- Check calendar for dates

## Mental Health Services

### Counseling Appointments

**Scheduling Counseling:**
1. Navigate to **Mental Health** → **Schedule Counseling**
2. Complete brief screening questionnaire (optional, helps with matching)
3. Choose:
   - **Specific Counselor**: View profiles and specialties
   - **First Available**: Quickest appointment
4. Select format:
   - **In-Person**: At Counseling Center
   - **Telehealth**: Video call
   - **Phone**: Audio only
5. Book appointment

**First Session (Intake):**
- 50-minute session
- Discuss concerns and goals
- Complete assessments (depression, anxiety screening)
- Develop treatment plan
- Schedule follow-up sessions

**Ongoing Counseling:**
- Typically 6-12 sessions per semester
- Weekly or bi-weekly
- Progress tracked
- Referral to off-campus provider if long-term care needed

### Crisis Support

**24/7 Crisis Hotline:**
- **Call**: (555) 999-HELP
- **Text**: CRISIS to 555-123
- Staffed by trained counselors
- Immediate support

**Crisis Walk-In:**
- Available during business hours
- No appointment needed
- Seen by crisis counselor within 30 minutes

**Emergency Situations:**
- If you or someone is in immediate danger
- Call Campus Security: (555) 999-0000
- Or 911

### Group Therapy

**Available Groups:**
- Anxiety management
- Depression support
- Grief and loss
- Mindfulness and meditation
- LGBTQ+ support
- Substance abuse recovery
- Eating disorder support
- Social skills

**Joining a Group:**
1. Attend information session (required)
2. Complete screening with counselor
3. Commit to attendance (6-10 weeks)
4. Attend weekly sessions

### Psychiatry Services

**Medication Management:**
- Evaluation by psychiatrist
- For conditions requiring medication (depression, anxiety, ADHD, etc.)
- Ongoing monitoring and adjustments
- Typically 15-30 minute appointments monthly

**Scheduling Psychiatry:**
1. Requires referral from counselor or primary care provider
2. Initial evaluation (45-60 minutes)
3. Follow-up appointments scheduled

## Telehealth

### Virtual Appointments

**When to Use Telehealth:**
- Minor illnesses (cold, flu, allergies)
- Follow-up appointments
- Prescription refills
- Mental health counseling
- Nutrition counseling
- Non-urgent issues

**Not Appropriate for Telehealth:**
- Emergency situations
- Physical exams requiring in-person assessment
- Lab work or imaging
- Procedures

### Using Telehealth

**Scheduling:**
1. Book appointment (select "Telehealth" option)
2. Receive email with video link
3. Test your setup (camera, microphone) before appointment

**During Appointment:**
1. Click video link 5 minutes before appointment
2. Join virtual waiting room
3. Provider admits you when ready
4. Consultation via video
5. Prescriptions sent to pharmacy electronically
6. Follow-up scheduled if needed

**Technical Requirements:**
- Computer, tablet, or smartphone
- Camera and microphone
- Stable internet connection
- Modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)

## Medical Accommodations

### Requesting Accommodations

**Medical Accommodations for Academics:**
- Extended exam time
- Excused absences for medical appointments
- Housing accommodations
- Dietary accommodations
- Assistive technology

**Process:**
1. Navigate to **Medical Accommodations** → **Request**
2. Fill accommodation request form:
   - Type of accommodation needed
   - Courses/situations affected
   - Duration needed (semester, ongoing)
3. Provider reviews and fills supporting documentation
4. Submitted to Office of Accessibility Services
5. Approved accommodations sent to instructors

**Processing Time:**
- Typically 5-7 business days
- Retroactive accommodations not granted (apply early)

### Housing Accommodations

**Medical Need for Housing:**
- Single room
- Air conditioning
- Ground floor
- Accessible features
- Emotional support animal

**Application:**
1. Submit through Health Portal
2. Provider documentation required
3. Review by Housing and Health Services
4. Approval and room assignment

## Health Insurance

### Student Health Insurance

**Automatic Enrollment:**
- All full-time students automatically enrolled
- Comprehensive coverage
- Can waive if have comparable coverage

**Coverage Includes:**
- Primary care at Health Center (no copay)
- Specialist visits ($15 copay)
- Mental health services (no copay)
- Prescriptions ($5-15 copay)
- Emergency room (20% coinsurance)
- Hospitalization
- Preventive care (no copay)

**Cost:**
- ~$2,500 per academic year
- Billed to student account

### Waiving Insurance

**Waiver Process:**
1. Navigate to **Insurance** → **Waive Coverage**
2. Available during waiver period (July-August for fall)
3. Provide details of alternate insurance:
   - Insurance company
   - Policy number
   - Coverage dates
4. Upload insurance card
5. Submit waiver
6. Approval within 3-5 business days
7. Charge removed from student account

**Waiver Requirements:**
- Coverage must be comparable
- Must cover local area
- Cannot be Medicaid/Medicare only

### Filing Insurance Claims

**For Services at Health Center:**
- Automatic billing to student health insurance
- No action needed from student

**For External Services:**
1. Navigate to **Insurance** → **File Claim**
2. Upload:
   - Itemized bill
   - Explanation of Benefits (EOB) if applicable
3. Reimbursement processed within 30 days
