# Career Services Portal - User Guide

## Overview

It integrates with employer databases and student academic records to deliver personalized career guidance.

## Getting Started

### Accessing Career Services

**For Students:**
1. Navigate to **Career Services** → **Career Portal**
2. Login with university credentials
3. First-time users: Complete your career profile

**For Alumni:**
1. Navigate to **Alumni** → **Career Services**
2. Login with alumni credentials
3. Access job listings and mentorship features

**Career Center Hours:**
- Monday-Friday: 9 AM - 5 PM
- Walk-in Hours: Tuesday & Thursday, 1 PM - 4 PM
- Virtual Appointments: Available 9 AM - 7 PM
- Email: careers@university.edu

### First-Time Setup

**Complete Your Career Profile:**
1. Navigate to **Profile** → **Career Information**
2. Fill required information:
   - **Personal**: Name, contact details, expected graduation date
   - **Academic**: Major, minor, GPA, relevant coursework
   - **Skills**: Technical skills, languages, certifications
   - **Interests**: Industry preferences, job type (full-time, internship, part-time)
   - **Work Authorization**: Citizenship/visa status, work eligibility
   - **Location Preferences**: Preferred cities, willingness to relocate
3. Upload your primary resume
4. Set job alert preferences
5. Submit profile

## Job Listings

### Browsing Job Postings

**Search and Filter:**
1. Navigate to **Jobs** → **Browse Listings**
2. View available positions with:
   - Job title and company name
   - Location and employment type
   - Salary range and requirements
   - Application deadline
3. Filter by:
   - **Job Type**: Full-time, part-time, internship, co-op
   - **Industry**: Technology, healthcare, finance, education, etc.
   - **Location**: On-campus, local, remote, national
   - **Salary Range**: Minimum/maximum hourly or annual rate
   - **Experience Level**: Entry-level, mid-level, senior

### Viewing Job Details

**Job Posting Information:**
- Company name and description
- Position title and department
- Employment type (full-time, part-time, internship, temporary)
- Location (on-site, hybrid, remote)
- Salary range
- Required qualifications and skills
- Preferred qualifications
- Application deadline
- How to apply (portal or external link)

### Applying for Jobs

**Submit Application:**
1. Click **Apply Now** on the job listing
2. Select resume to attach (or upload new)
3. Write or paste cover letter
4. Specify your availability
5. Provide reference contacts (if required)
6. Review application details
7. Submit application
8. Receive confirmation email

**Application Status Tracking:**
- **Pending**: Application submitted, under review
- **Reviewed**: Employer has viewed your application
- **Interview**: Selected for interview
- **Offered**: Job offer extended
- **Accepted**: Offer accepted
- **Rejected**: Application not selected

### Job Alerts

**Setting Up Alerts:**
1. Navigate to **Jobs** → **Job Alerts**
2. Create alert criteria:
   - Keywords, job types, locations
   - Salary minimums
   - Specific companies
3. Choose notification frequency (daily, weekly)
4. Save alert
5. Receive email notifications when matching jobs are posted

## Resume Management

### Uploading Resumes

**Add a Resume:**
1. Navigate to **Resume** → **My Resumes**
2. Click **Upload Resume**
3. Select file (PDF, DOCX, or TXT format)
4. Enter a descriptive name (e.g., "Software Engineering Resume")
5. Upload file
6. Resume is stored and available for applications

### Managing Multiple Resumes

**Resume Library:**
- Maintain multiple resume versions for different industries
- Set one resume as **Primary** (default for quick applications)
- Track which resume was used for each application

**Actions:**
- **Set as Primary**: Makes this your default resume
- **Download**: Retrieve a copy
- **Delete**: Remove outdated versions
- **Rename**: Update descriptive name

### Resume Review Service

**Request a Review:**
1. Navigate to **Resume** → **Request Review**
2. Select resume to review
3. Choose review type:
   - **General Review**: Overall feedback
   - **Industry-Specific**: Tailored to target industry
   - **Quick Check**: Format and grammar only
4. Submit for review
5. Receive feedback within 3-5 business days

**Resume Writing Tips:**
- Tailor each resume to the specific job
- Use action verbs and quantify achievements
- Keep to 1 page for students, 2 for experienced professionals
- Use consistent formatting and clear headings
- Proofread carefully before submitting

## Interview Scheduling

### Scheduling an Interview

**When Selected for Interview:**
1. Receive notification of interview invitation
2. Navigate to **Interviews** → **Scheduled Interviews**
3. View interview details:
   - Company and position
   - Interview type (in-person, phone, video)
   - Date and time
   - Duration (typically 30-60 minutes)
   - Location or meeting link
4. Confirm attendance

### Interview Types

| Interview Type | Format | Duration | Preparation |
|----------------|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Phone Screen** | Phone call | 15-30 min | Quiet location, resume in front of you |
| **Video Interview** | Zoom/Teams | 30-60 min | Test camera/mic, professional background |
| **In-Person** | On-site | 30-90 min | Arrive 10 min early, bring copies of resume |
| **Panel Interview** | Multiple interviewers | 45-60 min | Research each interviewer |
| **Technical** | Coding/skills test | 60-120 min | Practice problems, review fundamentals |

### Interview Preparation

**Career Services Offers:**
- **Mock Interviews**: Practice with career counselors
- **Interview Workshops**: Group sessions on techniques
- **Industry-Specific Prep**: Tailored guidance
- **Behavioral Question Bank**: Common questions and strategies

**Scheduling Mock Interview:**
1. Navigate to **Interviews** → **Mock Interview**
2. Choose format (in-person or video)
3. Select industry focus
4. Book available time slot
5. Receive feedback after session

## Career Events

### Browsing Events

**Upcoming Events:**
1. Navigate to **Events** → **Career Events**
2. View event calendar with:
   - Career fairs and expos
   - Employer information sessions
   - Networking events
   - Workshops and panels

### Registering for Events

**Event Registration:**
1. Click on event for details
2. View:
   - Event description and agenda
   - Date, time, and location
   - Participating employers
   - Capacity and availability
3. Click **Register**
4. Receive confirmation with event details
5. Add to your calendar

### Career Fair Preparation

**Before the Fair:**
- Research attending companies
- Prepare elevator pitch (30-second introduction)
- Print multiple copies of resume
- Dress professionally (business or business casual)
- Prepare questions for employers

**At the Fair:**
- Check in at registration desk
- Visit priority employers first
- Collect business cards
- Take notes after each conversation
- Follow up within 48 hours

## Alumni Mentorship Program

### Finding a Mentor

**Browse Mentors:**
1. Navigate to **Mentorship** → **Find a Mentor**
2. View alumni mentor profiles:
   - Name and graduation year
   - Current company and role
   - Industry and expertise areas
   - Bio and mentoring interests
   - Maximum mentee capacity
3. Filter by industry, expertise, or graduation year

### Requesting a Mentor

**Submit Mentorship Request:**
1. Select a mentor
2. Write introduction message:
   - Introduce yourself and your goals
   - Explain why you chose this mentor
   - Describe what you hope to learn
3. Submit request
4. Mentor reviews and accepts/declines
5. Once matched, schedule initial meeting

### For Alumni Mentors

**Becoming a Mentor:**
1. Navigate to **Mentorship** → **Become a Mentor**
2. Complete mentor profile:
   - Current position and company
   - Areas of expertise
   - Mentoring capacity (max mentees)
   - Availability
   - Bio describing your experience
3. Submit registration
4. Profile reviewed and approved
5. Begin receiving mentee requests

## Best Practices

1. **Keep your profile updated** - Refresh skills, GPA, and preferences each semester
2. **Tailor applications** - Customize your resume and cover letter for each position
3. **Start early** - Begin internship searches 4-6 months in advance
4. **Use multiple resumes** - Maintain industry-specific versions
5. **Follow up** - Send thank-you emails within 24 hours of interviews
6. **Attend events** - Career fairs and networking events are the top way to get hired
7. **Build relationships** - Connect with mentors and maintain professional contacts

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot apply for a job:**
- Ensure your career profile is complete
- Check that you have at least one uploaded resume
- Verify the application deadline has not passed
- Confirm you meet minimum requirements

**Resume upload fails:**
- Accepted formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT
- Maximum file size: 5 MB
- Ensure file is not password-protected
- Try a different browser if issue persists

**Interview link not working:**
- Check if the interview is virtual or in-person
- Verify date and time (check time zone)
- For video interviews, test your browser and camera
- Contact the employer or Career Services for assistance

**Event registration full:**
- Join the waitlist if available
- Check back for cancellations
- Attend alternative events in the same series
