# Scholarship Finder - User Guide

## Overview

The Scholarship Finder helps students discover, apply for, and track scholarships and financial aid awards. It provides a searchable database of available scholarships, tools for submitting applications, and tracking for awards, disbursements, and financial aid packages. Designed for students seeking financial support and administrators managing scholarship programs.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Scholarship Finder

**For Students:**
1. Navigate to **Financial Services** → **Scholarship Finder**
2. Login with university credentials
3. Browse available scholarships

**For Administrators:**
1. Navigate to **Administration** → **Scholarships & Financial Aid**
2. Full access to create, manage, and award scholarships

### Scholarship Finder Dashboard

**Dashboard Overview:**
- **Recommended Scholarships**: Matching scholarships based on your profile
- **Upcoming Deadlines**: Scholarships with approaching application deadlines
- **My Applications**: Status of your submitted applications
- **My Awards**: Scholarships you have been awarded
- **Financial Aid Summary**: Overview of your total financial aid package

## Searching for Scholarships

### Browsing Available Scholarships

**Scholarship Listings:**
1. Navigate to **Scholarships** → **Browse All**
2. View available scholarships:
   - Scholarship name
   - Award amount
   - Academic year
   - Application deadline
   - Eligibility summary
   - Status (open/closed)

### Filtering Scholarships

**Search Criteria:**
- **Amount**: Minimum/maximum award amount
- **Type**: Merit-based, need-based, athletic, departmental, external
- **Academic Year**: Target year
- **Major/Program**: Scholarships for specific fields
- **Eligibility**: GPA requirements, class standing, demographics
- **Deadline**: Only open scholarships
- **Keywords**: Search by name or description

### Scholarship Details

**Full Scholarship Information:**
- **Name**: Official scholarship name
- **Description**: Purpose and history of the scholarship
- **Amount**: Award amount (per semester, per year, or one-time)
- **Academic Year**: Applicable year
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Minimum GPA requirement
  - Major or program restrictions
  - Class standing (freshman, sophomore, etc.)
  - Financial need requirements
  - Other criteria (community service, leadership, etc.)
- **Application Deadline**: Last day to apply
- **Required Materials**: Documents needed for application
- **Status**: Active (accepting applications) or inactive

## Applying for Scholarships

### Submitting an Application

**Application Process:**
1. Click on the scholarship listing
2. Review eligibility criteria carefully
3. Click **Apply**
4. Complete the application form:
   - Personal statement or essay (if required)
   - Upload supporting documents:
     - Academic transcripts
     - Letters of recommendation
     - Financial need documentation
     - Resume or CV
     - Portfolio or work samples (if applicable)
5. Review all entries
6. Submit application
7. Receive confirmation email

### Application Requirements by Type

| Scholarship Type | Typical Requirements |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Merit-Based** | GPA, academic achievements, faculty recommendation |
| **Need-Based** | FAFSA, financial documentation, income verification |
| **Athletic** | Sports record, coach recommendation, physical exam |
| **Departmental** | Major-specific essay, faculty recommendation, portfolio |
| **External** | Varies by sponsor; may include interview |

### Application Status

**Tracking Your Application:**
- **Submitted**: Application received
- **Under Review**: Scholarship committee evaluating
- **Selected**: Chosen for the award
- **Not Selected**: Not chosen for this cycle
- **Incomplete**: Missing required materials

2. View all scholarships you've received:
   - Scholarship name
   - Award amount
   - Academic year/semester

**Full Award Information:**
- Scholarship name and description
- Award amount and payment schedule
- Academic year and term
- Conditions for maintaining the scholarship

**Maintaining Your Scholarship:**

- Maintain full-time enrollment status
- Complete any required community service hours

## Disbursements

### How Disbursements Work

**Payment Schedule:**
- Scholarships and grants are typically disbursed at the start of each semester
- Funds are applied to your student account first
- Remaining balance after tuition is refunded to you
- Refund methods: Direct deposit or check

### Tracking Disbursements

**View Disbursement History:**
1. Navigate to **Financial Services** → **Disbursements**
2. View all disbursements:
   - Award name
   - Disbursement date
   - Amount
   - Applied to account or refunded
3. Download disbursement records

### Refund Processing

**If Aid Exceeds Charges:**
1. Excess funds refunded after tuition is paid
2. Processing time: 5-10 business days from disbursement
3. Direct deposit (fastest) or check mailed
4. Set up direct deposit in account settings for faster refunds

## Best Practices

### For Students

1. **Apply early and often** - Submit applications well before deadlines
2. **Complete your FAFSA** - Required for most need-based aid; file annually
3. **Check eligibility carefully** - Save time by only applying for scholarships you qualify for
4. **Write strong essays** - Personal statements matter; tailor each one
5. **Get recommendations early** - Give recommenders at least 3 weeks notice
6. **Track deadlines** - Use the dashboard to monitor upcoming deadlines
7. **Maintain your GPA** - Many scholarships have GPA renewal requirements
8. **Check regularly** - New scholarships are added throughout the year

### For Administrators

1. **Post scholarships early** - Give students ample time to apply
2. **Set clear criteria** - Detailed eligibility requirements reduce unqualified applications
3. **Review promptly** - Students need timely decisions for financial planning
4. **Track fund balances** - Ensure scholarship funds are available before awarding
5. **Communicate decisions** - Notify all applicants, not just recipients

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot find matching scholarships:**
- Broaden your search criteria
- Remove filters one at a time
- Check if your profile is complete (for personalized recommendations)
- Look under different categories
- New scholarships are added regularly; check back often

**Application won't submit:**
- Verify all required fields are completed
- Check that all required documents are uploaded
- Ensure file formats are correct (PDF, DOCX)
- Check file size limits (typically 5 MB per document)
- Verify the deadline has not passed

**Award not appearing in financial aid:**
- Awards may take 1-2 weeks to process after notification
- Contact the Financial Aid office for status
- Check if any conditions need to be met first
- Verify your enrollment status is current

**Disbursement not received:**
- Check the disbursement schedule (typically start of semester)
- Verify enrollment status meets requirements
- Ensure direct deposit information is current
- Contact the Bursar's office for payment status
- Check if there are holds on your student account

**Scholarship was revoked:**
- Review the scholarship's renewal requirements
- Check your GPA against minimum requirements
- Verify you maintained full-time enrollment
- Contact Financial Aid to discuss options
- Apply for alternative scholarships
