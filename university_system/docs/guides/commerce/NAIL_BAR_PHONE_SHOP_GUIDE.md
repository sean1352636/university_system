# Nail Bar & Phone Shop - User Guide

## Overview

This guide covers two campus retail services: the **Nail Bar & Salon** (appointment-based nail treatments, waxing, and lash/brow services with technician management and loyalty points) and the **Phone Shop** (smartphone, tablet, and accessories sales with warranty tracking). Both integrate with the university finance system for student account payments, email receipts, and refund processing.

## Booking Appointments

### Booking Process

1. Navigate to the **Appointments** tab
2. Select one or more treatments from the menu (total price updates dynamically)
3. Choose a technician from the dropdown (or select "Any" for auto-assign)
4. Pick your appointment date (YYYY-MM-DD)
5. Select an available time slot
6. Click **Book Appointment**
7. System generates a unique booking reference (format: `NB-YYYYMMDDHHMMSS-###`)
8. Confirmation email sent automatically

### Appointment Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Booked** | Appointment scheduled |
| **Confirmed** | Customer confirmed attendance |
| **Checked In** | Customer arrived at the salon |
| **In Progress** | Treatment underway |
| **Completed** | Service finished |
| **Cancelled** | Appointment cancelled |
| **No Show** | Customer did not attend |

### Managing Appointments

- **Check In** — mark customer as arrived
- **Start Treatment** — begin the service
- **Complete Treatment** — finish the service
- **Cancel** — cancel the appointment
- **Reschedule** — change date, time, or technician

## Technicians

### Staff Profiles

Each technician profile includes:
- Name and Employee ID
- Specialties (e.g., Gel Nails, Nail Art, Acrylics)
- Certification details
- Phone and email
- Active/inactive status

### Availability

The system automatically checks technician availability when booking. Cancelled and no-show appointments free up the technician's schedule.

## Payments & Tips

### Payment Process

1. Select a completed appointment
2. Click **Process Payment**
3. Optionally add a tip amount
4. Select payment method:
   - **Cash** — pay at the counter
   - **Card** — debit or credit card
   - **Student Account** — deduct from university finance account (balance verified)
5. Confirm the payment
6. Receipt email sent automatically
7. Loyalty points added (1 point per £1 spent)

### Payment Breakdown

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Service Amount** | Total of all treatments |
| **Tip** | Optional gratuity |
| **Total** | Service + Tip |

---

## Loyalty Programme

- **Points**: Earn 1 loyalty point per £1 spent
- **Visit Tracking**: Total visits and last visit date recorded
- **Customer Profile**: Store preferred technician, favourite colours, nail type, allergies, and accessibility needs
- Points accumulate automatically with each payment

---

## Refunds - Nail Bar

1. Navigate to the **Refunds** tab
2. Search for a transaction by ID, appointment ID, or customer ID
3. Select the transaction and click **Process Refund**
4. Choose the refund method:
   - **Cash** — refund in cash
   - **Card** — refund to original card
   - **Student Account** — credit back to finance account
5. Refund reference generated (format: `NAILBAR-REFUND-YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`)
6. Refund receipt sent via email
7. Click **Export to CSV** to download transaction records

---

## Reports - Nail Bar

### Admin Report Contents

- **Today's Summary**: Appointments, transactions, service revenue, tips, total revenue
- **Weekly Summary**: Total appointments, revenue, tips, average service value
- **Top Treatments**: Top 5 treatments by bookings and revenue
- **Technician Performance**: Appointments handled, revenue generated, tips earned

Reports can be emailed to administrators.

---

## Best Practices - Nail Bar

1. **Book in advance** — popular slots fill quickly during term time
2. **Arrive on time** — late arrivals may need rescheduling
3. **Mention allergies** — update your profile with any sensitivities
4. **Combine treatments** — book multiple services in one visit for convenience
5. **Check loyalty points** — accumulate points for future rewards

---

## Troubleshooting - Nail Bar

**Cannot book appointment:**
- Ensure the technician is available on the selected date/time
- Verify all required fields are completed
- Try selecting a different time slot

**Payment failed:**
- If using student account, check your balance
- Try an alternative payment method

## Shopping & Checkout

### Adding to Cart

1. Select a product and set the quantity
2. Click **Add to Cart** (stock validated)
3. Click **View Cart** to review your items

### Checkout Process

1. Review cart items (Product, Price, Quantity, Subtotal)
2. Enter shipping address
3. View totals: Subtotal + 20% VAT = Total
4. Select payment method:
   - **Cash** — pay at the counter
   - **Card** — debit or credit card
   - **Student Account** — deduct from university finance account
5. Confirm purchase
6. Order number generated (format: `PHO-YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`)
7. Confirmation email with receipt sent
8. Stock levels updated automatically

---

## Warranty

All products include warranty coverage tracked by the system:
