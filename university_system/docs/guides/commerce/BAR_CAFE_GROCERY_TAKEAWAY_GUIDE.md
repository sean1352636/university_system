# Bar, Cafe, Grocery & Takeaway - User Guide

## Overview

This guide covers four campus commerce services: the **Bar** (alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages with age verification), the **Cafe** (hot drinks, pastries, and food), the **Grocery Store** (everyday essentials, fresh food, and household items), and the **Takeaway** (multi-restaurant food delivery and collection). All four integrate with the university finance system for student account payments, email receipts, and refund processing.

### Ordering Process

1. Select a restaurant from the list
2. Browse the menu and add items to your cart
3. Add **special instructions** per item (e.g., "extra sauce", "no onions")
4. Choose delivery type:
   - **Delivery** — delivery fee applies; address auto-fills from housing records
   - **Collection** — no delivery fee
5. Ensure your order meets the restaurant's minimum order amount
6. Select payment method: **Card**, **Student Account**, or **Cash on Delivery**
7. Confirm the order
8. Order status tracks from **Pending** → **Confirmed** → **Delivered**

### Dietary Information

Each menu item includes dietary labels:
- **Vegetarian** flag
- **Vegan** flag
- **Gluten-free** flag
- **Allergen** information

### Rating & Reviews

After delivery, you can:
- Rate your order (1-5 stars)
- Leave a written review
- Restaurant ratings are auto-calculated from order reviews

### Refunds

1. Search for the order transaction
2. Select refund method: **Cash**, **Card**, or **Student Account**
3. Refund processed with receipt email

## Best Practices

1. **Bar** — always have valid ID for age verification when ordering alcohol
2. **Cafe** — check daily specials for the best value
3. **Grocery** — look for per-product discount labels for savings
4. **Takeaway** — order above the minimum to avoid issues at checkout
5. **All services** — use your student account for convenient cashless payments
6. **All services** — keep your email updated to receive receipts

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Order failed:**
- If using student account, check your balance
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- For takeaway, verify the order meets the minimum amount

**Age verification failed (Bar):**
- Ensure you are 18 or older
- Contact bar staff for manual verification

**Delivery address missing (Takeaway):**
- Update your housing accommodation details in your profile
- Enter the address manually during checkout

**Product out of stock (Grocery):**
- Check back later for restocked items
- Try an alternative product
