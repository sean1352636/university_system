# Research Grants Management - User Guide

## Overview

The Research Grants Management system supports the full lifecycle of research activities, from project creation and grant applications to publication tracking and milestone management. Faculty, researchers, and administrators use this system to manage research projects, apply for funding, track team members, record publications, and monitor research equipment.

## Getting Started

### Accessing Research Grants

**For Faculty/Researchers:**
1. Navigate to **Research** → **Grants Management**
2. Login with university credentials
3. View your research dashboard

**For Research Administrators:**
1. Navigate to **Administration** → **Research Grants**
2. Full access to all projects, grants, and reports

### Research Dashboard

**Dashboard Overview:**
- **My Projects**: Active research projects
- **Pending Applications**: Grant applications awaiting decisions
- **Upcoming Milestones**: Research milestones with approaching deadlines
- **Recent Publications**: Latest published works
- **Funding Summary**: Total awarded vs. requested funding

## Research Projects

### Creating a Research Project

**New Project Setup:**
1. Navigate to **Projects** → **Create Project**
2. Enter project details:
   - **Title**: Full project title
   - **Principal Investigator (PI)**: Lead researcher
   - **Department**: Associated department
   - **Project Type**: Basic research, applied research, clinical trial, collaborative
   - **Start Date**: Project commencement date
   - **Budget**: Estimated total budget
   - **Description**: Abstract and objectives
   - **Keywords**: Research areas and topics
3. Save project
4. Project ID assigned automatically

### Managing Projects

**Project Actions:**
- **View Details**: Full project information and status
- **Edit**: Update project details, budget, or timeline
- **Add Team Members**: Assign researchers and roles
- **Link Grant**: Associate grant funding with the project
- **Track Progress**: Monitor milestones and deliverables
- **Close Project**: Mark project as completed

### Project Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Planning** | Project being developed, not yet started |
| **Active** | Currently in progress |
| **On Hold** | Temporarily paused |
| **Completed** | Successfully finished |
| **Cancelled** | Discontinued before completion |

## Team Management

### Adding Team Members

**Assign Researchers:**
1. Open the research project
2. Navigate to **Team** → **Add Member**
3. Enter team member details:
   - Name and ID
   - Role (Co-PI, Researcher, Graduate Student, Post-Doc, Lab Technician)
   - Contribution percentage (time allocation)
4. Save team member
5. Member added to project roster

### Team Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **Principal Investigator** | Project lead, overall direction and accountability |
| **Co-PI** | Shared leadership, specific research areas |
| **Researcher** | Conducts research activities |
| **Graduate Student** | Research assistant, thesis-related work |
| **Post-Doctoral Fellow** | Advanced research, mentoring |
| **Lab Technician** | Equipment operation, data collection |

### Managing Contributions

**Tracking Time Allocation:**
- Set contribution percentage for each member
- Ensure total allocation makes sense for project scope
- Track changes over project lifecycle
- Generate effort reports for compliance

## Grant Applications

### Applying for a Grant

**Submit Grant Application:**
1. Navigate to **Grants** → **Apply for Grant**
2. Link to an existing research project
3. Enter application details:
   - **Funding Agency**: NSF, NIH, DOE, private foundation, etc.
   - **Grant Program**: Specific program or call for proposals
   - **Requested Amount**: Total funding requested
   - **Application Deadline**: Submission due date
   - **Duration**: Grant period (start and end dates)
   - **Budget Justification**: Detailed breakdown of expenses
4. Upload supporting documents:
   - Research proposal
   - Budget spreadsheet
   - PI curriculum vitae
   - Letters of support
5. Submit application

### Application Status

**Tracking Your Application:**

```
Submitted → Under Review → Decision
                          ├── Awarded
                          ├── Declined
                          └── Revise & Resubmit
```

**Status Definitions:**
- **Draft**: Application in progress, not yet submitted
- **Submitted**: Sent to funding agency
- **Under Review**: Being evaluated by reviewers
- **Awarded**: Funding approved
- **Declined**: Funding not approved
- **Revise & Resubmit**: Revisions requested

### Grant Decisions

**When a Grant is Awarded:**
1. System records decision with:
   - Awarded amount (may differ from requested)
   - Grant period start and end dates
   - Decision date
2. Funds linked to research project
3. PI notified of award
4. Spending and compliance tracking begins

**When a Grant is Declined:**
1. Decision recorded with reviewer feedback (if available)
2. PI notified
3. Option to revise and resubmit if eligible
4. Application data retained for future reference

## Publications

### Recording a Publication

**Add a Publication:**
1. Navigate to **Publications** → **Add New**
2. Link to a research project
3. Enter publication details:
   - **Title**: Publication title
   - **Authors**: List of authors
   - **Type**: Journal article, conference paper, book chapter, technical report, thesis
   - **Journal/Conference**: Publication venue name
   - **DOI**: Digital Object Identifier (if assigned)
   - **Publication Date**: Date of publication
   - **Abstract**: Brief summary
4. Save publication
5. Project's publication count automatically incremented

### Managing Publications

**Publication Actions:**
- **View All**: List publications by project or researcher
- **Edit**: Update publication details or DOI
- **Link to Project**: Associate with additional projects
- **Export Citations**: Generate BibTeX, APA, MLA formats
- **Track Metrics**: View citation counts (if integrated)

### Publication Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Journal Article** | Peer-reviewed journal publication |
| **Conference Paper** | Presented at academic conference |
| **Book Chapter** | Chapter in edited volume |
| **Technical Report** | Institutional or agency report |
| **Thesis/Dissertation** | Graduate student work |
| **Preprint** | Pre-peer-review publication |

## Milestones

### Creating Milestones

**Set Research Milestones:**
1. Open the research project
2. Navigate to **Milestones** → **Add Milestone**
3. Enter milestone details:
   - **Title**: Milestone name (e.g., "Data Collection Complete")
   - **Description**: What this milestone entails
   - **Target Date**: Expected completion date
   - **Deliverables**: Expected outputs
4. Save milestone

### Tracking Progress

**Milestone Status:**
- **Upcoming**: Target date in the future
- **In Progress**: Work actively underway
- **Completed**: Milestone achieved
- **Overdue**: Past target date without completion
- **Cancelled**: No longer applicable

**Updating Milestones:**
1. Open the project → **Milestones**
2. Select milestone
3. Update status and notes
4. Record actual completion date
5. Save changes

## Equipment Management

### Registering Equipment

**Add Research Equipment:**
1. Navigate to **Equipment** → **Register New**
2. Enter equipment details:
   - **Name**: Equipment name/description
   - **Type**: Laboratory, computing, measurement, imaging, etc.
   - **Serial Number**: Manufacturer serial number
   - **Purchase Date**: Date acquired
   - **Cost**: Purchase price
   - **Location**: Lab or building location
   - **Associated Project**: Link to research project
3. Save equipment record

### Equipment Tracking

**Management Features:**
- View all registered equipment
- Track location and assignment
- Schedule maintenance and calibration
- Record repair history and costs
- Mark equipment as retired or transferred

## Best Practices

1. **Start applications early** - Begin grant applications at least 2 months before the deadline
2. **Keep records updated** - Log publications, milestones, and expenses promptly
3. **Track team contributions** - Maintain accurate effort allocation percentages
4. **Document equipment** - Register all research equipment with serial numbers
5. **Set milestone checkpoints** - Break large projects into manageable milestones
6. **Archive completed projects** - Maintain records for future reference and audits
7. **Monitor deadlines** - Use dashboard alerts for approaching grant deadlines

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot create a grant application:**
- Ensure a research project exists first (grants link to projects)
- Verify all required fields are completed
- Check that the application deadline is in the future

**Publication not linked to project:**
- Open the publication record
- Use the "Link to Project" option
- Search for and select the correct project
- Save changes

**Team member cannot be added:**
- Verify the team member has a valid university ID
- Ensure they are not already on the project
- Check that the project is in an active status

**Equipment registration failed:**
- Verify serial number is unique
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check that the associated project exists

**Grant status not updating:**
- Decision updates are typically performed by admissions staff
- Contact the Research Office if a decision has been made but not recorded
- Verify you have permission to update the application
