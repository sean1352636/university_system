# Medical Accommodation Templates

This directory contains standardized medical accommodation templates for the University Management System. These templates provide consistent, comprehensive accommodation plans for students with various medical conditions and disabilities.

## Available Templates

### MED-001: Chronic Illness Accommodation
For students with ongoing chronic illnesses (diabetes, Crohn's disease, lupus, etc.) requiring flexible attendance and assignment management.

### MED-002: Physical Disability Accommodation
Comprehensive accommodations for students with mobility impairments, paralysis, or dexterity limitations requiring accessible facilities and assistive technology.

### MED-003: Mental Health Accommodation
Support for students managing anxiety, depression, PTSD, bipolar disorder, or other mental health conditions affecting academic performance.

### MED-004: Temporary Injury Accommodation
Short-term accommodations for students recovering from injuries, surgeries, or acute medical events (typically 90 days or less).

### MED-005: ADHD/Executive Function Accommodation
Accommodations for students with ADHD or executive function disorders affecting focus, organization, time management, and impulse control.

### MED-006: Hearing Impairment Accommodation
Support for deaf or hard of hearing students, including interpreter services, captioning, assistive listening devices, and visual alternatives.

### MED-007: Vision Impairment Accommodation
Accommodations for blind or low vision students, including alternative format materials, assistive technology, and mobility support.

### MED-008: Learning Disability Accommodation
Support for students with specific learning disabilities (dyslexia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia, auditory processing disorders) affecting reading, writing, or math.

### MED-009: Pregnancy and Parenting Accommodation
Accommodations for pregnant students and student parents, including medical leave, lactation support, and flexible scheduling (protected under Title IX).

### MED-010: Chronic Pain/Fatigue Accommodation
Support for students with chronic pain conditions, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue syndrome, or similar conditions affecting stamina and concentration.

## Template Structure

Each template includes:

- **Template Name & ID**: Unique identifier for tracking
- **Description**: Brief overview of the accommodation purpose
- **Duration**: Typical length of accommodation (subject to individual review)
- **Features**: Quick reference flags for accommodation types
- **Specific Accommodations**: Detailed list of provided supports
- **Documentation Required**: Medical evidence needed for approval
- **Review Schedule**: How often the accommodation should be re-evaluated
- **Notes**: Additional guidance for implementation

## Usage

These templates serve as starting points and should be customized based on:
1. Individual student needs and medical documentation
2. Course-specific requirements
3. Available campus resources
4. Legal compliance requirements (ADA, Section 504, Title IX)

## Legal Compliance

All accommodations must comply with:
- Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
- Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act
- Title IX (for pregnancy-related accommodations)
- FERPA (student privacy)
- HIPAA (medical information privacy)
